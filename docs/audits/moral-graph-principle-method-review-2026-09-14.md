## Graph placement

The review extends `values-over-images` under `wisdom-first-principles`, rather than creating another badge. Its links compose identity non-attachment, compassionate constraint, equality, adherence/legitimacy separation, leadership continuity, goalpost integrity, friendship, and cost-bearing. `identity-view-and-non-attachment` supplies the identity distinction. `dream-integrity` now distinguishes protecting purpose from preserving every method or proving convictions through sacrifice.

The transferable distinction is between a principle, its present demonstration, and alternatives that may preserve conscience and responsibility. Defeat does not itself refute a principle; survival does not itself establish consent; admiration does not authorize political rule. Principles themselves remain open to ethical examination.

## Procedure and evidence boundary

The classifier can nominate `principle_method_tension` and recommend `separate_principle_from_method` from general observations, without character names or a supplied verdict. It asks:

1. What is the principle, and why is this method thought necessary?
2. What are the actual terms of each alternative, and what would each concede?
3. What protective or credible testimonial purpose remains?
4. Who bears costs, can consent or exit, and what responsibilities belong to other actors?
5. Which alternatives preserve conscience and remaining duties, and what would trigger reconsideration?

This is a relevance heuristic, not proof of psychological attachment or a recommendation to surrender. Its confidence value is not an empirical probability of moral correctness. Separate decisions must retain separate evidence. Life's worth is not conditional on usefulness or sacrifice. Aggressors' responsibility must not disappear into criticism of a resister.

The existing MCP handler carries the classification and recommended questions. Domain-evidence compression retains the five missing checks together so external-verification requirements cannot crowd out the final checks. All assistant-answer, terminal-eligibility, and execution-authority flags remain false. Objective attribution remains unresolved.

The earlier bare `wrong` cue could misread “Defeat did not make Bewcock wrong” as guilt. Guilt/repair relevance now needs explicit guilt/shame/blame/wrongdoing language or an action-specific admission, rather than that isolated word. This is a bounded cue repair, not a general-purpose negation parser.
