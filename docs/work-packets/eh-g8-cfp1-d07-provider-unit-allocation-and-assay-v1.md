## Required raw record and attribution

Capture provider project and UTC interval, source/build and deployment shape, provider meter name/unit/rate/version, gross units, account-level offsets/discounts, plan fee, prepaid opening balance, new paid or plan-funded balance, applied balance, closing balance, payable subtotal and vendor tax. Separately capture pseudonymous sponsor-term/revision and room-lineage IDs, room/member/program active intervals, lease ID/start/end/renewal cause, suspension and return events, first/last room-origin database request per lease, tagged request and response IDs, request counts/bytes and resource samples where available, native effect ID/status/postcondition, support/reconciliation minutes and expense category. Keep rejected and duplicate work in the **resource** record even when it consumes no successful-effect quota. Preserve pre-handoff and post-handoff sponsor terms separately; never move earlier consumption to the successor.

For each meter and interval, reconcile:

```text
project_gross_units    = background_units + attributed_sponsor_units + unattributed_units
project_gross_cost     = sum(project_gross_units × dated applicable gross rate)
resource_invoice_due  = all-project gross resource charges − applied pre-purchase
                        − discounts + resource-invoice tax
prepurchase_close     = prepurchase_open + separately paid additions
                        + plan-funded additions + documented adjustments − applied pre-purchase
period_cash_paid      = resource_invoice_cash_paid + separately paid additions
                        + separately verified plan/other invoice cash payments − cash refunds
```

The first equation is an **accounting partition**, not an assertion that provider units are natively sponsor-tagged. Replit's account invoice may include other projects, so compute project gross from CasimirBot-attributed lines and reconcile account cash separately. `resource_invoice_due` is the finalized obligation; `resource_invoice_cash_paid` is the amount actually settled in the declared period and may differ. The cash equations are a **period roll-forward**, subject to actual invoices/receipts and provider adjustments: a separately purchased balance is cash when bought, even if applied in a later period; a plan-funded allowance is counted through the plan payment, not as a second cash purchase. Record both the funding transaction and application period. Identify the funding source before allocating plan and resource costs so the same allowance is not counted twice in cohort contribution. Define rounding at the provider's meter resolution; an unexplained residual is `unattributed`, carried into an adverse/shared-cost range, never silently zero or charged to a random host. Pre-purchase and discounts change cash timing, not gross resource consumption or PBT reservations. Record whether vendor tax is recoverable and place nonrecoverable tax exactly once in the financial case.

Directly tagged request CPU/bytes and stored object growth may be assigned to their origin sponsor term after excluding the fixed/free baseline, including network or storage usage reported after room close. For database active time, derive a union of request-caused active intervals including [Replit's documented five-minute idle tail](https://docs.replit.com/features/data-and-storage/development-and-production) and compare it with baseline activity. **Do not sum overlapping tails as separate billable hours.** A causal tail can be billed after room close, expiry or host handoff; keep its reserved upper bound and eventual attribution on the origin sponsor term until reconciliation, without reopening effect admission or transferring the cost to a successor. Apply the same origin rule to delayed network/storage units. If minute-level provider or application evidence supports the union, allocate each incremental active segment among the concurrently causal sponsor terms by a declared rule, preserving total units; otherwise enter a lower/upper allocation range and carry the remainder as unattributed/shared cost. An always-active database floor belongs to B or fixed cost, not to every room. If the provider exposes only project aggregates, a paired B/P/T difference with bounded background variation can establish a cohort-level range, but it cannot establish an exact per-sponsor ledger.

Synthetic allocation fixture for the later meter implementation: with no background database activity, sponsor A's billable activity/tail is `[0,5)` minutes and B's is `[2,7)`. The union is **seven** billable minutes, not ten. An equal-share overlap rule assigns A 3.5 and B 3.5 minutes. If the B baseline already covers `[0,5)`, only `[5,7)` is incremental to hosted work under this fixture; those two minutes belong to B's causal tail, and the baseline five remain separately allocated. This fixture checks conservation and overlap behavior, not the actual Replit meter or a customer price.

Delayed aggregate provider usage cannot safely authorize an unbounded live room. CFP-3 must pre-reserve conservative per-operation and short room-lease budgets, including post-close causal tails and delayed network/storage usage, using independently enforced request, CPU/time, egress, storage and concurrency ceilings; settle from attributable application samples or an upper bound, then reconcile aggregate provider units later. The PBT ledger, native verified-effect quota and processor-payment ledger retain different identities and corrections. No provider invoice, reservation or billing event can create a successful native effect.

For the PBT conversion, reconcile the **term's cumulative fixed-point modeled cost** before rounding to whole internal tokens. A ledger that rounds each small request upward separately overstates cost-based consumption; a ledger that rounds each request down can silently admit unbounded sub-token work. The admission balance must include concurrent pending upper bounds, including post-close causal tails, and settle/release them atomically. Report the rounding residual as a bounded denomination effect when comparing settled PBT's monetary equivalent with modeled attributed variable cost; it cannot conceal missing provider units or an unexplained allocation residual. If actual provider rates change during a customer term, report the gross-cost variance separately from the term-pinned model and allowance; do not erase that operator exposure by retroactively changing the customer's PBT ledger. The fractional-cost and two-instance race cases in the [PBT contract](eh-g8-cfp1-hosted-resource-budget-token-contract-v1.md) are required implementation fixtures, not substitutes for observed B/P/T units.

## D07 decision output and rejection rule

For each B/P/T category report observed units or a sourced lower/upper bound, allocation method, background variation, support/refund/identity/payment assumptions and the matched private reference. Convert only attributable **variable** cost to PBT with the provisional versioned denominator; allocate base plan, free-personal cross-subsidy, nonconverting trials, support and incident reserve separately in the full cohort contribution. Show lower-paying/higher-trial and growth/concurrency cases, not just one isolated sponsor. The selected trial budget must permit the promised ten representative rights-cleared guest effects across seven continuous days; if it cannot, revise benefit, resource envelope or price before a customer promise.

Reject a D07 price/limit freeze when any material meter, baseline, provider tier, support, refund or trial-usefulness input is missing or silently zero; when each raw meter's accounted units cannot be reconciled to its matching provider units or bounded residual; when settled PBT's **dated monetary equivalent** differs from attributed gross variable cost without a documented rate, rounding, reservation/release or uncertainty adjustment; when a pre-dispatch reservation is below its conservative cost upper bound; when the proposed capacity cannot be enforced before hosted dispatch; or when gross resource use is confused with invoice cash due. Never compare token counts directly with heterogeneous CPU, request, byte or database-hour quantities. CFP-1 may use **sourced conservative bounds** in place of installed P/T measurement, with explicit uncertainty and a reviewer-checked financial case. CFP-3 still must prove the meter and denial paths on the selected installed artifact before paid/trial access is offered. This protocol selects no price, PBT allowance, customer limit or connector right.
