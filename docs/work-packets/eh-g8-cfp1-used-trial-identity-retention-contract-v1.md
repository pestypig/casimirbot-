# CFP-1 used-trial identity and retention contract v1

The owner [selected a one-time return rule](../evidence/eh-g8-codex-first-paid-product-delivery-v1/cfp-1/2026-09-19-trial-reuse-owner-selection-39.md): a host who consumed the seven-day no-card trial cannot get a new trial by deleting the CasimirBot account and returning with the **same verified Google/Auth0 sign-in identity**. This packet specifies the account/commerce seam needed to implement that rule. It does not select the [trial start trigger](../evidence/eh-g8-codex-first-paid-product-delivery-v1/cfp-1/2026-09-19-hosted-trial-start-proposal-50.md), exact accepted shared action, final trial cap, retention duration or privacy basis.

## Current source and the gap

The [read-only source map](../evidence/eh-g8-codex-first-paid-product-delivery-v1/cfp-1/2026-09-19-trial-account-identity-source-map-37.md) inspected an earlier revision; the same relevant paths were rechecked at `cc5a7a4c1ac956606aea756f59e6fcb0324a9f93` on 2026-09-19. `signInWebAccountSession` looks up an active `helix_account_linked_providers` row by `(provider, provider_subject)` and otherwise derives `${provider}:${providerSubject}`. `deleteAccountProfile` signs out sessions, revokes listed credentials and soft-deletes the account; it does not delete the provider-link row or establish a trial tombstone. `upsertAccount` clears `deleted_at` on profile conflict and can update the provider link. Migration 026 makes the linked-provider row dependent on the account on hard deletion. Migration 035 separately records issuer/audience for agent-link intents and issuer/tenant for agent bindings; the web linked-provider table itself does **not** store those namespaces. The current sandbox billing ledger is profile-keyed and credit-oriented, so it cannot prove this return rule.

Therefore a consumed marker attached only to the active profile row, existing provider-link row or current billing balance is insufficient. The system must resolve the one-time identity before any new trial enrollment, including after deletion, relinking, reinstall and restoration from older state. This is a future contract, not an assertion that the current source does it.

## Logical admission contract for CFP-3

1. A trial enrollment request must come from a current authenticated sponsor. The account service derives the provider identity from a verified login or verified explicit link, not an email, client-supplied subject, room, device or Stripe callback. Freeze a canonical provider namespace including the actual issuer/tenant distinctions required by the selected Google/Auth0 configuration before implementation; migrate or explicitly map the current `(provider, subject)` rows without claiming they already contain issuer/tenant. Reject ambiguous namespace mappings.
2. Maintain a separate logical consumed-trial index that survives the permitted account deletion and any hard deletion of the ordinary account row. Its lookup key is a versioned, keyed digest of the canonical verified provider namespace and subject, or another privacy-reviewed equivalent; do not put raw email or display name in that index. A keyed digest remains linkable personal data, not anonymity. The record needs only the reviewed anti-reuse purpose, trial-policy version, consumed state, lifecycle timestamps/revision and audit/retention metadata. Keep customer-facing room, node, credentials, source content and billing details out of it.
3. Under one serializable or equivalently fenced transaction, check **all currently verified identities linked to the sponsor** and the sponsor's existing trial state, then create at most one authoritative start/end and consumed index entry set. A concurrent second start, second device or retry returns the same result and does not extend the clock. A trial-start failure before durable commit grants nothing; an uncertain response is resolved by reading the authoritative record. [Selection 106](../evidence/eh-g8-codex-first-paid-product-delivery-v1/cfp-1/2026-09-20-cfp1-recommended-direction-owner-selection-106.md) chooses explicit Start Trial after rights-cleared readiness; exact identity/retention implementation remains pending qualified review.
4. A later verified provider link must reconcile trial history in the same protected transition. If either linked identity or account has a consumed trial, the resulting account cannot start another. Linking must never merge another person's room, paid subscription, consent, node or program grants. If a link conflicts with another active account or has ambiguous history, reject it and require reviewed account recovery rather than silently assigning a trial. Unlinking a provider cannot erase its consumed marker or give the remaining account a second start.
5. Deletion must revoke or fence current sessions and hosted authority under the separate account/room contract. A subsequently verified identical provider identity may enter a fresh account/session, but its used trial remains consumed; historical room membership, invited-guest status, owner grant, subscription, node, consent and effect lease never reappear merely from the marker. The consumed index is **denial evidence only**, not an entitlement or an account-recovery credential. A matching email with a different verified provider subject does not match this index. The policy cannot establish that two unlinked provider identities belong to the same person; do not infer that from email.
6. Trial and link decisions must read a current revision from the authoritative store. On store outage, key unavailability, uncertain link state or stale restore, deny new hosted trial admission and expose a typed reconciliation state. Restore/replay must not lower the consumed revision or revive an expired trial. Keep independently authorized free personal tools and authenticated stop/revoke available through their own checks.

This proposal requires one atomicity design across sign-in/link, trial and deletion state; a separate digest table by itself is not enough. CFP-3 must choose transactional ordering, locking/idempotency keys, key-version rotation and migration rules, then prove them with database-backed fixtures. A replayed OAuth callback or a signed-in browser with an old profile ID must not bypass the identity check.

## Retention decision still required

The account/privacy owner and qualified reviewer must decide the narrow purpose and basis for retained anti-reuse evidence, exact retention period and expiry/deletion process, user notice, access/export/deletion response, processor/storage boundary, key custody and rotation, backup/restore handling, and treatment of provider unlink or account recovery. An indefinite marker is **not** authorized by this packet. Conversely, automatic expiry of the only marker would contradict the owner's selected same-identity return rule if the trial could restart afterward. If those requirements cannot be reconciled, the product owner must revise the one-time policy or the trial offer before CFP-3 dispatch; implementation cannot silently choose either outcome.

The privacy decision must also say whether a historical verified link to a deleted account may be retained as the minimum marker. Ordinary account deletion must communicate the reviewed exception accurately. The [current-source deletion map](../evidence/eh-g8-codex-first-paid-product-delivery-v1/cfp-1/2026-09-19-account-deletion-authority-source-map-59.md) shows why the existing `deleteAccountProfile` response and storage flow do not establish this disclosure or full dependent-data teardown.

## Acceptance handoff

Use existing DOM-02/03/05/06 and ENT-01/03/05/08 scenarios, with positive and negative fixtures for: one start under concurrent requests; retry after uncertain response; same verified Google or Auth0 subject after deletion; original and previously linked provider after deletion; link and unlink before/after consumption; same email with a distinct verified subject; two distinct issuers or tenants with colliding subject strings; duplicate/replayed callback; conflicting active account link; signed-out and stale-profile calls; failed transaction and store/key outage; restored old snapshot; expiry; and fresh paid authorization after trial. Each fixture should record the verified namespace, account/trial revision, expected denial or single clock, and proof that no room/program authority or second native effect was obtained. A no-card trial creates no checkout, card collection, credit posting or automatic paid conversion.

**Stage:** CFP-1 remains active (specification); CFP-2/3 remain blocked. The owner-selected return rule is captured, while privacy/retention review, trigger, exact benefit and installed entitlement evidence are still missing. This packet admits no production trial or commercial claim.
