## Return format and decision rule

For **each** C01–C15 row, return: `artifact/version or bounded source rule`; `planned treatment`; `rights holder/source of authority`; `license/terms and edition/date`; `evidence reference`; `disposition: permitted / permitted with conditions / permission required / exclude / unresolved`; `conditions and required notices or source offer`; `customer claim/channel covered`; `reviewer and review date`; and `change trigger`. Mark inapplicable obligations expressly rather than leaving blank. For a conditional item rejected or still unresolved, identify the required owner decision and build exclusion/replacement; silence never converts a condition to permission. Record first-party claims separately from third-party permissions and privacy/service terms.

R-MC-01 additionally needs a context-by-context disposition for free personal, no-card trial, paid guest action, expiry/refund and host handoff. A generic mod-license review does not answer that business relationship. Reviewer conclusions must cite the actual design and current terms or written permission scope; the product owner then freezes the approved C01–C15 treatment, operation IDs, claim text and channel. CFP-3 later verifies exact signed EXE, separately provisioned JAR/profile, public kit, updater and notice bytes against those decisions. Final signed hashes are output evidence from CFP-3, not a prerequisite to requesting this planned-component review.
