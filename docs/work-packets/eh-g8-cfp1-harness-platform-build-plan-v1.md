On 2026-09-14 the owner directed this task to concentrate on the harness platform. Minecraft remains an evolving proof of concept: its detailed behavior will be developed through the existing game and NAV work. The [product goal](../architecture/casimirbot-environment-harness-product-goal-v1.md) defines the intended system; [CFP-1](eh-g8-cfp1-product-rights-and-offer-contract-v1.md) controls product and commercial decisions; the [work program](../helix-environment-harness-work-program-v1.md) alone controls active stages and capability maturity. This is a working platform specification, not another stage ledger. Runtime implementation continues to require its existing admission.

The supported capability set will develop with evidence. Record intended capability families now, refine exact operations in bounded implementation packets, and freeze the selected release's advertised set before evaluation/publication. Do not equate today's Minecraft primitives with the eventual personal product, or advertise a planned operation as available. The integrated acceptance questions are whether the group can correct and question the shared AI while the sole principal makes useful progress; whether each owner can withdraw or take over without waiting for that principal; and whether provider work occurs and is charged only in an explicitly admitted service mode. Rehearse failure of voice, helper, principal delivery, player connection and sponsored capacity as separate transitions. The [work program](../helix-environment-harness-work-program-v1.md) alone determines when runtime slices may be admitted and which maturity claims their evidence supports.

Sequence these as two explicit tracks. For the selected release, CFP-1 freezes the free personal and paid hosted offer, CFP-2 proves ordinary external-client personal use, and CFP-3/4 prove subscribed collaboration and integration without funded inference. The owner selected an [action-inclusive first paid benefit](../evidence/eh-g8-codex-first-paid-product-delivery-v1/cfp-1/2026-09-19-shared-actions-benefit-owner-selection-26.md), so that hosted release also needs at least one exact guest-requested, owner-granted action with technical and commercial-rights acceptance; a read-only room rehearsal alone cannot close it. The proposed assisted-room track is conditional on a later owner offer decision: specify service admission first, qualify a read-only/replayed shared conversation, then one controlled player, the existing multi-node prerequisites, and finally simultaneous players under a new conflict contract. NAV-EQ may independently qualify one actor under its parallel G8 lane; a technical game rehearsal does not admit a CFP-2 product release or a funded room service.

## Governed skill operations — 2026-09-19 alignment

The intended environment tool is an addressable, bounded **skill operation** over the existing capability, temporal-plan, connector, and evidence machinery. A principal selects an objective, target, policy, effect ceiling, deadline, and completion conditions; Helix admits that exact request. A qualified domain implementation may plan or repair finite segments inside it, while the existing arbiter and scheduler enforce authority and release. The skill does not invent a mission or acquire a second reasoning loop. This is a specification for future qualification, not a claim that a generic skill-operation endpoint exists.

| Responsibility | Platform contract |
| --- | --- |
| Meaning and applicability | Versioned mechanics, prerequisites, effects and limitations help the principal select a skill. Stored knowledge is not evidence that its preconditions hold now. |
| Bounded execution | The qualified implementation derives route or behavior segments only inside the admitted objective, traversal and effect policy. Each segment binds current observation, parent revision, authority, implementation version, predecessor checkpoint and expected starting state; it is revalidated before dispatch. |
| Evidence and recovery | One stable operation reference links the mission, actor, request, child segments, checkpoints, actual effects and uncertainty across reconnects. Retaining the record does not retain execution permission. Resume requires fresh identity, lease, evidence and native state checks. |

For substantial work, a supported client should receive the operation reference and actual admission state promptly, then inspect progress, pause reasons and measured completion separately. Client task-handle extensions may carry that reference when negotiated; they neither extend authority nor replace Helix's operation identity or native postconditions. Ordinary status access must remain possible for a supported client without the extension.

Progress, physical control, evidence freshness and reasoning delivery are separate dimensions. A request awaiting principal pickup does not establish that the player is held; a cancellation request does not establish control release. Causal reason records should identify the actor, operation and revision, observed condition, applicable rule, measured effects, control state and next decision owner. In the proposed assisted-room mode, shared GPT Live may explain a permitted reason; a restricted worker may analyze observations or propose an alternative; the one qualified principal revises the mission; and each owner can stop or revoke their own contribution directly. Neither conversation nor a read-only helper may turn a camera or player under the label of observation.

The [reasoning architecture](../architecture/helix-environment-agent-reasoning-v1.md) owns the role and lifecycle rule; [Environment Time](../architecture/helix-environment-time-action-planning-v1.md) owns parent/child derivation, watermarks and stale-segment rejection; [NAV](eh-g8-environment-spatial-navigation-v1.md) owns movement policy, local repair and feedback; [federation](eh-g8-shared-room-multi-host-capability-federation-v1.md) owns actor grants and cooperative dependencies; [exact binding](eh-g8-pna3-4-exact-reasoning-binding-voice-pickup-v1.md) and the [voice contract](../architecture/voice-service-contract.md) own truthful event delivery and speech. These are existing owners, not a new service family.

The next bounded **game qualification** is one controlled actor pursuing an admitted movement objective under NAV-EQ and its existing prerequisites. The proposed assisted-room integration later lets the shared conversation inspect a real pause reason, request non-mutating route analysis and submit an attributed revision to the exact principal. Freeze its artifact, source identity, objective, policy, lease, observations, numerical limits, failure cases and postconditions before dispatch. Preserve NAV-EQ's three rolling extensions, changed-condition repair, interruption, reconnect, revocation and stale-effect rejection. Cooperative simultaneous movement is a later separately admitted qualification; the current federation contract serializes player mutations. The game qualification does not require the proposed paid AI mode or change CFP-2's blocked status.

The first scenario can use a cave approach with a water crossing, subject to the owning NAV packet's accepted fixture and live conditions. The movement/stop cases inform NAV-EQ; shared AI, worker and principal-delivery cases belong to the conditional assisted-room track. The cases below are proposed platform acceptance, not recorded gameplay results or a new gate order:

| Event | Required observed result | Decision boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Player encounters water under a no-swimming policy | The proposed water segment is rejected; the controller reports the measured checkpoint, actual control state, governing prohibition and effects so far. | A hard prohibition cannot be replaced by a cheaper route cost. |
| Current evidence supports a dry detour inside the admitted envelope | Qualified local repair publishes its changed corridor and causal evidence; the derived segment passes fresh authority/state checks before dispatch. | No principal pickup is required solely to narrate or approve a permitted local repair. |
| No qualified dry detour remains | Only the admitted hold/release behavior occurs and the operation reports a decision needed. | The implementation does not add swimming, mining, a new destination or an unseen route. |
| A room participant asks why the player stopped | GPT Live explains the current reason with its source and uncertainty. | A reason read does not become a new action or assert the principal's private motive. |
| A helper evaluates another route | The proposal carries the observation and mission revision used, without moving the player or camera. | Late or stale analysis cannot be spoken as current fact or executed. |
| The group requests a materially different route | The request is attributed to the exact principal; delivery/pickup and resulting decision are recorded separately. | Only the principal's admitted revision changes the group plan. |
| Owner stops or revokes during any of the above | The direct control path stops/releases according to its existing bounds and records actual native outcome; subsequent stale segments are rejected. | No conversation, worker or model pickup is needed for owner control. |

Retain procedural knowledge, operation/task history and fresh current-state evidence as distinct inputs. A past route or failed attempt may suggest a candidate, but cannot certify current geometry or authority. Learned motion/search components may propose under a versioned qualified profile; they receive no direct environment writer. Personal and hosted reasoning profiles use the same environment semantics only after each connection and continuation mode is separately qualified. The native planner receives deadlines and authority loss as control facts; provider credentials, subscription data and payer choice stay in their own account/service layer.

## How the Minecraft proof of concept informs the platform

This task does not build courses, issue gameplay actions or retest the other task's live session.

The platform contract should remain stable while individual program capabilities improve. Freeze source inputs and test scenarios for each evaluation slice, capture its exact outputs, then revise the next slice openly when new evidence requires it. Faster replay with extra privileges is not ordinary live-operation evidence.
