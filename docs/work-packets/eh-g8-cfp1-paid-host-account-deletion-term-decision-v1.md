# CFP-1 paid-host account deletion term decision v1

**Current owner direction:** [selection 106](../evidence/eh-g8-codex-first-paid-product-delivery-v1/cfp-1/2026-09-20-cfp1-recommended-direction-owner-selection-106.md) chose ordinary cancellation at paid-period end and an immediate account-deletion admission fence. The later [D04–D06 lifecycle owner freeze](eh-g8-cfp1-d04-d06-lifecycle-owner-freeze-v1.md) controls the exact first-pilot cancellation, fresh-proof deletion, 24-hour handoff, refund-base and same-identity-return terms. The alternatives and unanswered language below preserve the pre-freeze proposal history. Qualified financial/privacy review must still settle tax, pending invoices, disputes, notice and retained records before implementation or customer copy. Current source does not implement this term.

The [current deletion source map](../evidence/eh-g8-codex-first-paid-product-delivery-v1/cfp-1/2026-09-19-account-deletion-authority-source-map-59.md) shows that `DELETE /api/account/profile` currently signs out and soft-deletes selected account data but does not cancel a subscription, close rooms, revoke all related connector/device authority or prove local effect release. The selected product has one paying host sponsoring authenticated guests, with explicit handoff to another eligible host. Account deletion must therefore define *both* the customer money outcome and the room/effect outcome. The owner selected a provisional D06 direction above; the current sandbox credit ledger is not a paid hosted-term implementation.

Stripe's [current cancellation guide](https://docs.stripe.com/billing/subscriptions/cancel) distinguishes immediate cancellation from `cancel_at_period_end`; the latter lets the customer use the paid period and can be reversed before period end. It also distinguishes proration/credit from an actual refund, notes that pending invoice items or usage may still need separate handling, and identifies `customer.subscription.updated` and `.deleted` events. The [API cancellation reference](https://docs.stripe.com/api/subscriptions/cancel) says immediate cancellation stops further subscription renewal but does not by itself settle every pending invoice item. These are provider behaviors to test against the selected account configuration, not a finding about the user's live Stripe account or a refund entitlement.

## Provisional unused-time payment-refund calculation for D11 review

For the selected **single flat recurring sponsoring-host charge**, use the actual settled subscription charge for the current paid service period **after discounts and excluding separately identified pass-through tax**, in the payment currency's integer minor units (`B`). Do not use the list Price, an unpaid invoice, a credit balance, a free trial, or a receipt from another sponsor. Obtain the period's authoritative start `s` and end `e` from verified processor/account entitlement evidence with `e > s`. Define effective deletion time `t` as the durable server timestamp at which the first accepted account-deletion request **fences new hosted admission**. Retries and a delayed processor callback retain that same `t`. Clamp `t` to `[s,e]`; compute unused seconds `U = e − t` and total period seconds `L = e − s` in the same unit. This uses the actual paid period, not a fixed 30-day approximation.

```text
base_target_minor = ceil(B × U / L)
additional_base_refund_minor = min(
  B − prior_confirmed_base_refunds_minor,
  max(0, base_target_minor − prior_confirmed_base_refunds_minor)
)
```

Here `prior_confirmed_base_refunds_minor` covers only refunds of the **same settled subscription charge** already confirmed by the processor; a requested, failed or merely credited amount is not counted as money returned. Require `0 ≤ prior_confirmed_base_refunds_minor ≤ B`, one idempotent deletion/refund identity and a cap at the charge's remaining refundable balance. All arithmetic is integer/rational until the final ceiling to the customer's benefit. Do not deduct payment-processor fees from this provisional customer base. If the charge is disputed, unpaid, already fully refunded or the processor cannot confirm its refundable balance, fence hosted work at `t` but show a separate **refund pending review** state instead of issuing or promising an amount. A previous voluntary period-end cancellation does not change `t` if the customer subsequently requests immediate account deletion before `e`.

**Pending same-charge refund fence:** `pending`, `requires_action` or otherwise nonterminal refunds against this charge are neither confirmed cash returned nor free refundable balance. Before creating the deletion refund, serialize all app-origin refunds for that charge under one durable revision, re-read processor refund/charge state, and **do not submit a new deletion refund while any other same-charge refund remains nonterminal**. Keep the deletion's original `t` and a truthful pending-review receipt. After the earlier refund succeeds, fails or is canceled with verified processor state, recompute the formula from the same original `B`, `s`, `e` and `t`, subtracting only the confirmed base refunds. The deletion refund itself retains one idempotency identity while nonterminal; retries inspect its status instead of issuing another request. An out-of-band Dashboard refund or dispute discovered during reconciliation also pauses automatic submission and requires a fresh processor comparison. A local lock cannot prevent an external Dashboard action, so the implementation must detect that race and escalate rather than assert an exact payout from stale local state. [Stripe's refund guidance](https://docs.stripe.com/refunds) documents partial refunds and pending, failed, canceled and action-required states; it does not make a local receipt proof of payment return.

| Hypothetical test, no tax | Expected additional base refund | What the case checks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| $10.00 settled for exactly 30 days; deletion at day 10; no prior refund | $6.67 | `ceil(1000 × 20/30)` cents, using elapsed seconds and customer-favoring minor-unit rounding. |
| Same charge and deletion, with $1.00 already confirmed as a same-charge **base** refund | $5.67 | Subtract the confirmed earlier refund once; do not refund a total greater than the $6.67 target. |
| $8.00 settled after a discount for exactly 30 days; deletion at day 15 | $4.00 | Use actual paid base, not the undiscounted list Price. |
| Any settled charge; deletion at or after the paid-period end | $0.00 | No unused paid time remains; the deletion still fences and reconciles authority. |
| $10.00 settled, deletion at day 10, a separate $1.00 same-charge refund still pending | **Pending review; no second refund submitted yet** | If the $1.00 later succeeds, recompute to $5.67 additional; if it verifiably fails or is canceled, recompute to $6.67, using the original deletion time. |

Tax charged to the customer and any legally required tax reversal/refund are **separate** from `B` and these example amounts; the customer must not be shown the base figure as a final total until the qualified New York/customer-jurisdiction and processor review resolves that treatment. Pending metered items, partial credits, disputes, nonstandard promotions, currency minor-unit rules, actual refund eligibility and processor failure need explicit qualified dispositions. [Stripe's cancellation guidance](https://docs.stripe.com/billing/subscriptions/cancel) distinguishes cancellation/proration credit from money returned, and its [refund API](https://docs.stripe.com/api/refunds/create) is a separate operation; neither source proves that this calculation is configured or lawful for the selected offer. CFP-3 must prove the reviewed formula, cancellation and refund as separate idempotent transitions in a sandbox, including **two same-charge refund requests racing, a nonterminal prior refund that later succeeds or fails, duplicate deletion retries and an out-of-band processor refund**, before any customer copy or payment path uses them.
