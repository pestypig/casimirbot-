# CFP-1 D12 integrated claim prefreeze v1

The [D04–D06 lifecycle owner freeze](eh-g8-cfp1-d04-d06-lifecycle-owner-freeze-v1.md)
is the controlling product-owner input for trial, handoff, cancellation and
deletion values. Final D12 must reproduce those values or identify the exact
qualified D11 condition that changes them. Their selection closes an owner
choice; it does not enable a CTA, satisfy D11 or prove CFP-3/4 behavior.

This packet converts the owner's standing instruction to use the recommended
product choices into a **provisional first-release claim set**. It resolves the
choice of which FC rows to carry forward; it does not supply the qualified D11
answers or constitute the owner's final post-review D12 acceptance. All CTAs
remain disabled until the row's enable rule and later installed evidence pass.

## Provisional commercial wording

The first D07 validation candidate was **USD $20 per month per sponsoring
host**. [Integration review 309](../evidence/eh-g8-codex-first-paid-product-delivery-v1/cfp-1/2026-09-21-cfp1-d07-envelope-integration-review-309.md)
rejects that amount under the current conservative assumptions. The
independently reviewed next validation candidate is **USD $60 per month per
sponsoring host**; [review 310](../evidence/eh-g8-codex-first-paid-product-delivery-v1/cfp-1/2026-09-21-cfp1-revised-60-dollar-term-independent-review-310.md)
accepts its arithmetic and fail-closed boundary, not a final or public price.
One room, one invited verified guest, one connected program and up to **50
verified successful owner-granted shared actions per paid term** remain the
provisional validation shape, with no paid overage, token pack, credit balance
or automatic upgrade. Customer copy uses successful
shared actions as the understandable capacity unit. Internal PBT and provider
units remain cost/admission accounting and must never be described as GPT/model
tokens or a transferable customer balance.

Use a U.S.-located sponsoring purchaser and U.S.-located invited guest as the
provisional first paid/trial market target for D11 and D07 validation. This is
not public availability copy until the exact location evidence, travel/change
policy, product-tax treatment and processor configuration are qualified. Free
personal MCP eligibility is independent of the hosted market decision.
