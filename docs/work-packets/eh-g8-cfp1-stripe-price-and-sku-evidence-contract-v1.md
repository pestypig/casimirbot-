## Authorized account fact capture

For **each** existing candidate, an authorized account operator records the following non-secret fields from a dated read-only Stripe Dashboard or API view, with its account/mode identified in a private evidence reference. Do not copy API keys, customer/payment data, session cookies or raw account exports into the repository. Mark a field `unavailable` rather than inferring it from a product name or fixture. Stripe's [Price object](https://docs.stripe.com/api/prices/object) exposes the fields below; the [Product object](https://docs.stripe.com/api/products/object) provides product name and default Price. Their `livemode` flags distinguish test from live objects.

| Field group | Exact fact to record | CFP-1 decision use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Identity and mode | Price `id`, `livemode`, `active`; Product `id`, `name`, `active`, `default_price` | Distinguish an existing test Price from its live counterpart and an active candidate from an archived one. A Product may have multiple Prices; name/default status alone is not a benefit selection. |
| Transaction role | Actual selected direct-processing or separately contracted merchant-of-record arrangement for the hosted subscription; dated account/contract evidence for CFP-1, with later CFP-3 test transaction/receipt evidence; named merchant, seller and statement/receipt identity; responsible tax, refund, dispute and support party | A hosted Checkout URL, Product or Price does not establish that Stripe is the merchant. Cost the selected arrangement and obtain D11's role allocation before approving D07 fees or D12 seller copy. Mark the production role `unavailable` until verified. |
| Charge shape | Price `type`, `billing_scheme`, `recurring.interval`, `recurring.interval_count`, `recurring.usage_type`, `unit_amount` or `unit_amount_decimal` **when per-unit**, `currency`, and any `currency_options` in scope. For tiered or variable prices also capture `tiers`, `tiers_mode`, `transform_quantity`, `custom_unit_amount` and the checkout quantity/usage rule as applicable. | Confirm recurring rather than one-time/prepaid behavior, billing interval and offered currencies. Compute the actual possible charge from the selected pricing scheme and quantity/usage rule; a null `unit_amount` does not mean zero. Record the currency's minor-unit interpretation rather than assuming every amount is dollars or cents. |
| Tax and trial coupling | Price `tax_behavior`, Product `tax_code` if set, Price `recurring.trial_period_days` if set, and any separate configured checkout/subscription trial behavior | Freeze customer tax presentation and make sure the selected seven-day no-card hosted trial is not silently converted into a card-collecting or automatically billable Stripe trial. A Price field alone does not prove the account's complete tax or trial policy. |
| Customer location and automatic tax | Selected paid-checkout market, purchaser location evidence/validation rule, applicable seller registrations and account-mode tax settings; for a test subscription and invoice capture `automatic_tax.enabled`, `automatic_tax.disabled_reason`, the location source and charged tax result at checkout and renewal | A zero tax result or paid invoice alone does not prove correct tax treatment. [Stripe's location contract](https://docs.stripe.com/tax/customer-locations) says missing/invalid renewal location can automatically disable tax while a subscription invoice still finalizes. D11 must settle the geographic/tax policy; CFP-3 must detect and reconcile this event, including address change and missing-location fixtures. Do not infer the customer's use location solely from the seller's New York address or the room sponsor's IP. |
| Commercial terms | Selected hosted benefit/room limits, geographic offer, refund/cancellation wording, fee schedule applicable to the actual account/payment mix, and source/date for expected hosting, trial, support and dispute costs | Cost the candidate $5/$10 or another selected price over the same billing period. A Price object does not prove margin, eligibility, rights or customer benefit. |

Stripe documents the [pricing model and Dashboard fields](https://docs.stripe.com/products-prices/manage-prices). A created Price's amount and recurrence cannot be edited in place, so a mismatch on those fields needs a new matching Price after owner selection. The current [Price update API](https://docs.stripe.com/api/prices/update) permits several limited updates, including setting `tax_behavior` while it is `unspecified`; once set to `inclusive` or `exclusive`, that field cannot be changed. An existing Price with the wrong locked tax behavior, charge scheme, product association or other unchangeable term also needs a new Price or an explicit exclusion from the selected offer. No old object is accepted merely because its display name matches $5 or $10. This packet does not authorize Price creation or modification.

## Stage-specific freeze

**CFP-1 closes the commercial specification**, not the live payment integration. Before closure, the owner selects one pilot hosted benefit, customer amount **or bounded charge formula with quantity/usage rule**, currency, recurring interval, tax presentation, paid limits and cancellation/refund/deletion terms after the [cost worksheet](eh-g8-cfp1-hosted-offer-owner-decision-brief-v1.md#cost-and-price-evidence-required-for-o-03o-04) has a dated assumption for each input. Capture existing test/live Price facts when an authorized read is available. If a matching live Price does not yet exist, record `not provisioned` with the exact intended fields and assign creation plus re-read to CFP-3.COMMERCE; a production Price ID is not a prerequisite to **specifying** CFP-1. Do not mark an existing object verified merely because its ID is present in a local environment variable or historical fixture.

**CFP-3 accepts the actual payment mapping** only after the selected test Price and separately authorized live Price, if production activation is later admitted, match the frozen amount or charge formula, currency, interval, product/benefit and tax behavior. The commerce owner must inspect the configured Price before checkout, bind the verified Price and Product IDs to the correct account mode, and reject wrong-mode, inactive, one-time, prepaid, wrong-product, wrong quantity/usage or stale mapping. A test Price is never a live Price; a successful checkout redirect is not a hosted entitlement. The no-card trial has its own authoritative start with no Price, Checkout Session, card or credit posting. Production Price creation, checkout and charging require their separate stage and owner authorization.

The D11-reviewed market and tax-location rule is also a new-purchase condition: obtain and validate the purchaser location before charging, reject an unsupported or unrecognized location, and reconcile any difference between site evidence and the address collected by Checkout rather than treating an arbitrary zero calculation as approval. At renewal, a missing location may already have produced a finalized/paid invoice, so preserve the observed payment and term facts, flag automatic-tax disablement for operator reconciliation and follow the reviewed customer policy; do not silently mark tax compliance, auto-revoke a paid entitlement or issue a refund solely from that tax anomaly. This is a specification for CFP-3 fixtures, not a configured Stripe Tax claim.
