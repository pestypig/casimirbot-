## What current source does and does not establish

At source HEAD `256554ca2637b2978a83616d9f9670069fdfd8c4`, `shared/helix-shared-realtime-room.ts` limits a room to two participants. The base `user` policy locks shared rooms; the public-room experiment requires both an enabled server setting and a stored account policy already enrolled for rooms, while nondeveloper source ingress has additional conditions. These are experimental policy checks, not subscription admission or proof of free personal reachability. `shared/helix-billing-entitlement.ts` exposes a sandbox credit-oriented entitlement with `starter_monthly` and prepaid-credit checkout targets. Existing sandbox event handling is reuse material, but it is not a hosted-room SKU or proof of the reported $5/$10 Stripe Price objects. The [current payment-to-Minecraft-effect source map](../evidence/eh-g8-codex-first-paid-product-delivery-v1/cfp-1/2026-09-19-payment-to-minecraft-effect-source-map-15.md) traces how room account policy gates the inspected player-action path while billing remains separate. The [September 8 code/deployment audit](eh-g8-cfp1-code-deployment-alignment-audit-v1.md) observed one Replit Autoscale deployment and a production database connection label; it did not measure per-customer cost, actual database authority, live Stripe configuration or paid-room throughput. The current two-person cap happens to match the provisional host-plus-one-guest trial target, but does not enforce trial eligibility, establish a paid-tier limit or prove three-person acceptance.

## Cost and price evidence required for O-03/O-04

Measure at declared active personal users, **active trial hosts**, paying hosts, rooms, members and connected nodes. Separate costs shared by free personal access from **incremental hosted** costs, including trial rooms that earn no subscription revenue; a zero-priced personal download does not make required identity or transport free to operate. The [personal connection checkpoint](../evidence/eh-g8-codex-first-paid-product-delivery-v1/cfp-1/2026-09-19-free-personal-connection-dependency-17.md) identifies the current local/remote path and measurement cases; neither route is yet accepted for an ordinary user. The [dated public-price reference](../evidence/eh-g8-codex-first-paid-product-delivery-v1/cfp-1/2026-09-19-public-unit-cost-reference-19.md) gives an illustrative amount remaining after published Stripe fees for the owner's reported $5/$10 amounts and identifies missing account-specific Replit/Auth0 costs; it is not a margin finding. Use one accounting period matching the proposed Stripe interval and record source, unit, date, observed volume and uncertainty for each input:

| Input | Evidence source to capture | Current state |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Identity/account and production database | Deployed provider invoice or metering, storage/backup/restore plan, support for Google/Auth0 linking | Deployment label and source architecture exist; per-active cost and production authority are unverified. |
| Room coordination and network | Replit deployment resource/usage invoice, room connection time, tunnel egress and concurrency measurements under the selected limits | One deployment shape was observed in the earlier audit; bill and per-room load are unverified. |
| Binary delivery and maintenance | Download/update egress, signing, artifact storage, incident and security-update reserve | No approved public installer/feed or measured customer distribution cost. |
| Payment | Actual processor contract/fee schedule, tax handling, disputes, refund rate and failed-payment recovery | Source has sandbox checkout/webhook foundation; current Stripe account Price objects and effective fees are unverified. |
| Support and privacy operations | Attended pilot support minutes, loaded labor cost, retention/export/deletion and incident reserve | No external-user pilot measurement. |

For each candidate price, calculate `period revenue − processor/tax/refund costs − paid-host variable cost − no-card trial-host variable cost − allocated fixed service cost − support/incident reserve`. Show the result at both the intended and a lower paying-host count, plus plausible trial-to-paid conversion and the free-personal cross-subsidy requirement. Do not declare the reported $5 or $10 option viable from fixture amounts or a room-capacity constant. CFP-5 later validates actual willingness to pay and support load; CFP-1 must at least freeze the cost assumptions and price decision before CFP-3 checkout implementation.
