## Cohort and measurement record

Use **one declared period equal to the proposed subscription interval**. If the proposed interval is monthly, reconcile a completed monthly provider invoice and usage period; a partial-period dashboard or a tax-inclusive portal amount cannot silently stand in for a monthly unit rate. For every entry record `source`, `observed_at`, `period_start/end`, `currency`, `unit`, `observed_value or unavailable`, `lower/upper bound`, `allocation rule`, `private evidence reference` and `reviewer`. Mark the measurement `missing` when neither an observation nor a sourced conservative bound exists.

| Case | Workload held fixed or varied | Minimum measured counters | Derived purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| B — personal baseline | Service plus representative free personal activity, **no sponsored room**; same artifact, client and provider plan as the other cases. | Project gross resource units and cash charges; identity/database/storage/backup, traffic, support and distribution allocation; personal users and duration. | Shared baseline and free-personal cross-subsidy, not zero cost. |
| P — paying sponsor | B plus one eligible host, one guest, one connected program and representative accepted shared actions for the proposed paid capacity. | Room-hours, member-hours, program-hours, successful and failed/duplicate/uncertain effects, compute/egress/storage increments and support time. | `P − B` attributable incremental paid-host load, plus explicitly allocated shared cost. |
| T — no-card trial sponsor | B plus the selected seven-day trial ceiling: provisionally one room, one guest, one program and up to ten **verified** effects, including retries, expiry and unresolved outcomes. | Same counters as P, trial starts, completed/expired trials, held capacity, support/reconciliation time and post-expiry storage/retention. | `T − B` attributable incremental trial load, charged against converting and nonconverting cohorts alike. |

Run comparable windows or normalize by the observed room/member/program hours **and state the normalization**. A difference from two unrelated days is not a causal marginal estimate unless background and development activity are identified and bounded. Development-AI usage and other projects on the Replit account must not be allocated to customer hosted operation. If P/T cannot be measured before CFP-1, name a **sourced conservative upper bound per unmeasured input** and show how it affects the next `$60` validation candidate and historical rejected `$20`/`$10`/`$5` comparators; otherwise D07 remains open. Do not run a production customer cohort under this worksheet.

## Calculation and decision rows

For each candidate amount or bounded charge formula, calculate in a private working sheet using one explicit tax/refund basis. The charge used in `paid_revenue` is **gross before refunds, excluding customer-collected pass-through sales tax**. Subtract customer refunds exactly once in `payment_cost`, also excluding returned pass-through tax. Include nonrecoverable provider/vendor tax exactly once in the P/T variable or allocated fixed cost to which it belongs; track its actual cash timing separately in the provider reconciliation. Recoverable tax is recorded separately and is not an operating cost unless review establishes otherwise. Allocate the shared/base portion of identity, database, support and other costs to `allocated_fixed_cost` **only if it was not already included** in the P/T marginal costs; reconcile each expense to exactly one term.

```text
paid_revenue         = sum(gross pre-refund charge excluding customer sales tax for each paying host)
payment_cost         = sum(actual-or-bounded processor fees, disputes and refunds excluding returned sales tax)
paid_variable_cost   = sum(P − B attributable cost for each paying host, including allocated nonrecoverable vendor tax)
trial_variable_cost  = sum(T − B attributable cost for each trial start, including allocated nonrecoverable vendor tax)
allocated_fixed_cost = hosted share of plan, identity, database, delivery, support and incident reserve
                       not already in P/T, including remaining nonrecoverable vendor tax not assigned to P/T
period_contribution  = paid_revenue − payment_cost − paid_variable_cost
                       − trial_variable_cost − allocated_fixed_cost
```

Show at least an **intended** and **lower paying-host** case, with a separate **higher trial-start** sensitivity and declared trial-to-paid conversion. Do not count converted trials as free to serve, and do not assume free personal operation has no shared cost. Show both gross-resource economics and actual-cash timing; a prepaid balance can make current cash due small without making the service sustainable. An upper-bound case that is negative does not automatically reject a price, but it must be visible to the owner before selection. A missing fee, support or marginal-load input cannot be silently entered as zero.

For each case, declare `free_personal_users`, `trial_starts`, `paying_sponsors`, `peak_simultaneous_rooms`, `connected_programs` and the selected action mix. The primary P/T case must follow the [first-party domain guest route](eh-g8-cfp1-hosted-guest-request-ingress-v1.md) through two account sessions, invite/join, request/owner approval, native result and return; charge identity, domain API, notification/polling, history and reconciliation use to the correct baseline or sponsor bucket. Do not substitute a guest-owned Codex/MCP-only case for a browser guest without a model account. Test at least a low-paying/high-trial cohort, the intended first cohort and a growth cohort that crosses any known deployment or support-capacity step. The $10 comparator's 10-paying/30-trial example is one scenario, not a scalable per-user unit rate. Recalculate shared database active-time overlap and tails, compute bursts, service-tier/base-plan changes, support load and trial conversion for each cohort. Show both (a) total period contribution and (b) contribution per paying sponsor after the shared/free/trial allocation. Reconcile underlying resource quantities to provider gross units and money totals to cash due without double counting. Mark a tier threshold or concurrent-load cost `missing` when no measurement or sourced conservative bound covers it. A sponsor PBT cap does not by itself bound aggregate platform spending or prove that more subscribers improve margin.
