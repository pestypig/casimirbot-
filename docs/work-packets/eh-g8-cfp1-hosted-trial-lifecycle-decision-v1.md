### Provisional room-slot and idle-suspension rule for D07/D12 review

Treat the provisional **one active room** limit as one sponsored room identity occupying a slot while it is open, including when its finite activity lease is idle or suspended. Suspension stops routine room work and new effects but does not close the room, free its slot, extend the seven-day clock, release pending effect capacity or reset sponsor PBT. An explicit room close frees the concurrent room slot; a later room receives a new identity but the same sponsor-trial clock, used successful-effect count, pending effects and cumulative PBT. A browser close, device switch or room recreation cannot reset those account-level counters. This is the preferred first-cohort accounting rule to assay and obtain owner/qualified-review confirmation, not an implemented or final customer limit. Guest and program replacement semantics, exact idle interval, close/history retention and customer notice remain D07/D11/D12 decisions.

At an explicit eligible-host handoff, atomically check that the successor's own term has room capacity before accepting the same room identity. Close the predecessor's admission interval and keep its earlier effects, PBT reservations and later-billed causal resource use with that origin; the successor starts a distinct term interval and receives no predecessor trial time, capacity, action grant or model account. A failed handoff leaves the room suspended or closed under the reviewed recovery rule, never active under two sponsors. Owner stop/revoke and native release remain available independently of either room slot.
