# CFP-1 D07 hosted budget workload fixture v1

The [platform resource-budget contract](eh-g8-cfp1-hosted-resource-budget-token-contract-v1.md) makes PBT an **internal accounting and admission unit** within a fixed subscription, not a sold model token or customer credit. The [D07 assay](eh-g8-cfp1-d07-provider-unit-allocation-and-assay-v1.md) calls for matched baseline (B), paid (P) and trial (T) provider units. This packet fixes a repeatable T10/P10 workload before anyone converts rates into an allowance. Its event times and visit counts are **engineering samples**, not promised room-hours or a selected customer quota. Substitute the exact rights-reviewed Minecraft or non-game action without changing the account, room and cost boundaries; if the action has materially different resource needs, rerun the workload. The proposed [first-party owner-granted offer/guest-request/result API](eh-g8-cfp1-first-party-guest-action-api-contract-v1.md) supplies the browser route trace to count; it is not implemented or finally frozen.

## T10: ten effects across a usable seven-day trial

Use one verified sponsoring host `H`, one separately verified guest `G` with **no required GPT/Codex account**, one owner-controlled program, one first-party domain room and one selected bounded native effect. Keep the hosting build, provider deployment shape, database configuration, selected action version and baseline load fixed across B and T. The host signs in six days before trial start and the guest signs in three days before trial start. Under the **proposed, not approved** seven-day web-session value, the host must reauthenticate at trial day one and the guest at trial day four. Record both old-session denial and same-subject return under the [cross-clock fixture](../evidence/eh-g8-codex-first-paid-product-delivery-v1/cfp-1/2026-09-20-cfp1-session-trial-cross-clock-contract-226.md). If D02 selects a different lifetime, preserve the principle and schedule at least one independent session crossing inside T10.

At `t=0`, show the ready benefit and limits, explicitly Start Trial once, then create the **new non-model first-party room**, bind one invitation to the server-resolved verified guest subject, accept it under that same subject, create a pending one-program share and confirm it through the separately authenticated signed local installation. Inspect the permitted room/program state before any action offer. Count lookup, room create/inspect, invite/accept, pending-share/confirm, CSRF/device proof, database/egress and wrong-invitee or wrong-device denial work; none starts a model provider. For **each** of the ten proposed effects, the owner explicitly creates a fresh exact offer with a one-use grant for that guest, the guest retrieves the permitted offer and submits one request under current admission checks, and the guest or owner reads its final result. Exercise those ten distinct pre-granted one-use effects at trial hours `0, 12, 30, 48, 72, 96, 120, 144, 156, 167`; verify each native postcondition and request/owner attribution. This distribution forces late-trial usefulness, two account returns, repeated offer/grant/lease work and history retention. At hour 168, attempt a new hosted effect and record expiry denial without native release; owner stop/revoke, result inspection and free personal access remain independently available. After one request settles, retry its original `POST /action-requests` with the same actor, `offer_id`, idempotency key and body; verify that the original `request_id`/status returns without a second native effect or duplicate settlement of that work identity. Count the repeat transport work separately. Do not infer ten effects merely from API success: if the selected connector cannot produce all ten verified results, mark T10 **missing**, not passed.

For the proposed API, record **each** `POST /action-offers` (which creates the exact grant), `GET /action-offers`, `POST /action-requests`, `POST /action-offers/:id/revoke` when used, and `GET /action-requests/:id` with actor, response state, bytes, CPU/request units, database work and originating sponsor term. The clean T10 fixture uses one listed-offer fetch and one final-result read per visit, so its **scripted successful-effect flow alone** is 40 browser API calls for ten effects: 10 owner offer/grant creations, 10 listings, 10 guest requests and 10 result reads. Its required settled-request retry adds work beyond those 40 calls. The count also excludes first-party room create/inspect/invite/accept, verified-invitee lookup, pending-share/local confirmation, authentication/CSRF, target-candidate acquisition, lease renewal, connector delivery, notifications, background presence, intermediate status checks and provider tails; measure or conservatively bound every omitted category separately. A real UI may batch listings or push results, but D07 may change this scripted route trace only with a versioned observed alternative, not by pricing each native success as one HTTP call. Count pre-creation denials even though they have no `request_id`; count durable pending/uncertain result reads and owner revocation without counting a verified effect. Preserve retries as actual request work even when they return an idempotent receipt.

After each intentional visit, leave the room open but stop renewing its finite active lease. In at least one interval, leave both browser tabs open and backgrounded; in another, close both tabs. In each, measure whether database-backed presence/refresh, compute, egress and storage work actually stop after the **proposed** idle boundary, while the room still occupies its provisional slot. On return for permitted inspection, require current account, sponsor, room membership and program visibility; a fresh owner action grant is required only for a **new effect**, along with connector and PBT admission. A revoked or exhausted guest may still inspect allowed history under its separate read policy. Do not reset the trial, effect or PBT ledgers. Record attributable pretrial host/guest sign-in and account setup units as a separate acquisition/setup bucket with its matched B background, then measure trial-start through the last provider-billable causal database tail, delayed network/storage use and the selected retained-history period. Allocate each expense once in the cohort case; do not omit pretrial work or charge reused account setup to every term. Attribute a post-expiry causal tail to its originating sponsor term without reopening action admission. The selected idle duration, history retention and guest/program replacement policy remain D07/D11/D12 inputs.

Record three distinct T10 variants against the same trace schema:

| Variant | Work and result | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| T10 clean | Ten intentional visits, ten verified effects, one trial start, independent host/guest reauthentication, expiry and one idempotent replay. | Minimum useful action-inclusive trial resource demand under the proposed finite-lease path. |

Under `cfp1.hosted_shared_action_budget.v1`, the first 12 admitted leases may contain ten verified effects and two costed denial/no-effect visits; visits 13–30 must be expected lease-exhaustion denials before ordinary room work. No pending outcome is silently counted as a success or free capacity. Run an explicitly sustained-open room as a separate adverse case rather than silently averaging it into T10 clean. If routine polling persists despite suspension, the finite-lease bound is invalid; use measured/sourced sustained-open units and revise the offer or control before choosing PBT. The ten-effect target must survive the selected cost ceiling **and** independent CPU/request/egress/storage/concurrency bounds, including normal invite, owner pre-grant, return and reauthentication work. No automatic overage charge or token pack repairs a failed T10.

## P10 and paid-growth comparators

Repeat the first-party host/guest/program path under one verified **full monthly paid term** `M`, measured from authoritative term start through renewal or expiry and the selected retained-history tail. P10 uses ten visits with one verified effect each at fractions `0, .05, .15, .25, .40, .55, .70, .80, .90, .98` of `M`. P50 and P100 each use 25 visits at fractions `0.98 × j/24` for `j = 0…24`, with respectively two or four separately owner-granted, requested and verified effects per visit. Under the scripted one-listing-per-visit and one-result-read-per-effect flow, the **successful-effect portion alone** makes 40, 175 and 325 browser API calls for P10, P50 and P100 respectively: three per effect for owner offer/grant creation, guest request and result read, plus one offer listing per visit. For each paid tier, add one pre-native denied attempt and one accepted no-effect attempt per ten verified effects; those mandatory attempts raise the actual run's route total above the successful-effect portion. The counts also exclude all other route, authentication, connector and background work named in T10. Run unresolved-native uncertainty separately rather than silently replacing an effect. Record the actual paid interval, each visit's finite lease and its causal provider tail, month-end renewal or expiry admission, payment failure if applicable, and post-term inspection/retention cost. These are **engineering load points**, not selected paid effect limits. Keep effect count, attempted visits, actual room-active intervals, retention and setup units separate. Test peak simultaneous sponsors at intended, lower-paying/higher-trial and growth cohorts; shared database active-time tails are a provider **union**, not one full copy per host. A subscription amount can be chosen only after the [unit-economics worksheet](eh-g8-cfp1-hosted-unit-economics-worksheet-v1.md) carries B/P/T variable costs, base-plan/free-personal allocation, support, identity, payment/refund, tax and trial starts. The `$10/month` and lower-price comparisons are assays, not selected customer prices.

For every run, retain pseudonymous sponsor term and room lineage, account/session transition, grant/effect key, intended and observed lease interval, attempted and verified effects, pending/no-effect reconciliation, raw provider meter units, dated rate, cumulative modeled cost, cumulative PBT debit and concurrent reserved upper bounds. Reconcile raw units to project totals and the paid/trial cohort while preserving background/unattributed residuals. A candidate PBT allowance must admit the **peak cumulative settled cost plus all outstanding conservative reservations** needed for the selected useful journey; it cannot be derived from the average successful effect alone. Report cost and useful completion for clean, waste, uncertainty and sustained-open cases separately. If a category is unmeasured, use a sourced conservative bound or keep the numerical D07 freeze open.
