The treatments above are **owner-selected provisional directions**. “Include conditionally” means the item is presently selected by broad build rules or needed by the contemplated experience, but it cannot ship until the named conditions are satisfied. “Exclude” is the product direction for the first customer cohort, not a claim that the inspected current build already excludes every corresponding byte. The owner must freeze the final treatment, bounded version/source rule, first customer claim and channel for each C01–C15 row after qualified review; that reviewer must record origin/license/notice/permission disposition for each approved component and the paid Minecraft relationship. CFP-3 then tests the exact signed EXE plus separately provisioned JAR/profile and public package/feed against the final matrix, rejecting unexpected inclusions and omissions. The current broad `dist/**`/`dist/public` selectors do not themselves implement this allowlist.

The selected free personal scope and paid/trial action register still require exact operation IDs and installed evidence. This sheet neither changes the canonical [CFP-1 stage](../helix-environment-harness-work-program-v1.md) nor admits CFP-2/3.
