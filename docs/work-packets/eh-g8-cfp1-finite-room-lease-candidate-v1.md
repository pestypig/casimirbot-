# CFP-1 finite-room lease candidate v1

The [unattended-room source audit](../evidence/eh-g8-codex-first-paid-product-delivery-v1/cfp-1/2026-09-20-cfp1-open-browser-room-cost-source-audit-212.md) found that the current **experimental** room can continue database-backed work after its dialog closes. The first-party no-model guest room does not yet exist. [Replit's publishing/database billing guide](https://docs.replit.com/billing/about-usage-based-billing), rechecked 2026-09-20, says production database compute remains active for five minutes after the last request; its Autoscale compute and request meters are separate. The [D07 assay](eh-g8-cfp1-d07-provider-unit-allocation-and-assay-v1.md) compared five- and fifteen-minute nonrenewed visits; its [account-rate](../evidence/eh-g8-codex-first-paid-product-delivery-v1/cfp-1/2026-09-20-cfp1-finite-lease-account-rate-screen-220.md) and [compute-plus-database](../evidence/eh-g8-codex-first-paid-product-delivery-v1/cfp-1/2026-09-20-cfp1-finite-lease-compute-screen-222.md) screens are partial-resource sensitivities. They support testing a finite lease, not choosing a customer price or capacity.

## Candidate for the next D07 test

Use a **five-minute active room lease** as the first engineering and customer-usability candidate. Room creation or a deliberate authenticated return obtains one lease after current sponsor-term, account/session, membership, room-slot and PBT/resource admission. An explicit participant action while active may request renewal; eligible actions include **Continue room**, the owner's explicit pre-grant, submitting the selected guest request, or sending a room message **if messaging enters the selected first-party scope**. For a renewal accepted at authoritative server time `t`, set `expires_at = t + 5 minutes`, replacing the old deadline rather than adding five minutes to it. Reserve only the incremental bounded work and the **union** of any extended causal database tail, while accounting separately for the renewal request itself; overlapping tails are not charged as independent full tails. A successful renewal receives one new room revision. Replayed or parallel renewals under the prior revision return the committed state or deny, without extending time or capacity twice. The clock never renews merely because a tab is visible, a client polls, a presence heartbeat arrives, a response is rendered or a network retry occurs. Either current authorized participant may deliberately renew, but the sponsor can see consumption and stop the room; a guest has no payment or program-grant authority by renewing.

At lease expiry, mark the shared room **suspended** and stop routine database-backed room/frame/presence refresh at both client and service boundaries. Do not show either participant as live-present solely because their tab remains open. The one-room trial slot remains occupied until explicit room closure; closure and recreation do not reset trial time, verified-effect count, pending reservations or cumulative PBT. An intentional authenticated return may request a new lease and see only permitted current state after sponsor capacity, account/session, membership and program visibility are rechecked. Returning to a room does not submit an action or revive an owner grant. A new effect requires its own current exact grant, connector and safety checks.

An already admitted native effect follows its **separate finite action deadline, owner grant, stop and reconciliation rule** even if the room suspends, the browser signs out or the hosted term expires. Suspension does not cancel that action by assumption, duplicate it, manufacture a successful result or turn an uncertain effect into available quota. Idempotent result inspection and owner stop/revoke remain reachable under their own authorization even when the room is suspended or PBT is exhausted; they must have independently bounded resource cost. The first-party room may use another transport, but it must deliver these same observable states and cost bounds.

The page must show the active lease's approximate remaining time, that the room pauses when its **time-limited lease** expires unless someone deliberately renews it, how **Continue room** reserves more hosted capacity, and whether exhaustion, expired eligibility or lost membership prevents return. Reading without an eligible renewal action may therefore end in suspension even while the page is foregrounded; test that state and provide a clear, accessible renewal control. Trial wording must distinguish this five-minute **candidate room lease** from the continuous seven-day trial clock and ten verified-effect target. Do not publish the number or an implied unlimited-room promise until D07/D11/D12 approve the actual behavior and CFP-3/4 prove it.

## Acceptance and rejection

Run the [T10 clean, waste and uncertainty workload](eh-g8-cfp1-d07-hosted-budget-workload-fixture-v1.md) across seven days, plus P10/P50/P100, foreground active pair, foreground reading **without renewal**, both tabs backgrounded, both closed, one unattended open tab, explicit early and parallel renewal, suspension, return, expiry and host handoff. For the candidate, assert no routine room-origin database request or new room lease after the authoritative five-minute deadline without a separately recorded deliberate renewal. Verify that one accepted renewal at `t` sets one `t + 5 minutes` deadline and reserves only incremental, nonduplicated work/tail across overlapping intervals. Measure provider CPU-seconds, GB-seconds, requests, egress, production-database active-time **union including the five-minute idle tail**, storage and retained-history cost; identify baseline overlap and unexpected background requests. Verify that ten separate approved guest effects remain feasible and understandable, including host/guest session return and a grant requested near lease expiry. Failed renewals and denial/status/safety traffic still have costs and must be bounded.

The simple no-background, ten-separated-visit database-time screen is at most **100 minutes** only if each visit uses one five-minute lease, all room-origin database work stops at expiry, no visit overlaps another, and the provider's five-minute tail applies as modeled. Thirty such visits have a **300-minute** screen. These are conditional database-time envelopes, not a provider bill, PBT allowance or limit on compute, egress, storage, support or repeated renewals. If a live trace violates the stop condition, if the ten-effect trial is unusable, or if whole-offer cost and per-resource admission cannot be bounded, reject this candidate. Compare an explicitly costed fifteen-minute or sustained-open alternative, or revise the proposed benefit and price before D07 selection. CFP-3 later implements the selected rule; CFP-4 proves it on the frozen installed cohort. This packet changes no runtime or production setting.
