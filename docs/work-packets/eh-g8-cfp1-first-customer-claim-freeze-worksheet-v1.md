# CFP-1 first-customer claim freeze worksheet v1

The [CFP-1 exit rule](eh-g8-cfp1-product-rights-and-offer-contract-v1.md#delegation-and-handoff) requires an acceptance test for **each selected claim** before CFP-2/3 dispatch. The [claims register](eh-g8-cfp1-claims-evidence-register-v1.md) preserves the twelve full G8 obligations and current claim holds. This worksheet makes the narrower first-customer copy/CTA contract reviewable without replacing G8 or declaring a launch. [Selection 106](../evidence/eh-g8-codex-first-paid-product-delivery-v1/cfp-1/2026-09-20-cfp1-recommended-direction-owner-selection-106.md) establishes product targets; the later [D12 prefreeze](eh-g8-cfp1-d12-integrated-claim-prefreeze-v1.md) provisionally carries FC-01–09 forward under the owner's standing recommendation instruction. Its sentences remain disabled candidate claims pending the controlled D11/D07 variables and final post-review owner acceptance. Every operation in final wording must have its own row or an explicit subcase; a broad phrase cannot be accepted by one convenient tool call.

For each accepted row, freeze a specification manifest **before execution**: exact customer sentence and CTA state; operation/verb and context IDs; client build/profile; account/room/owner/source/subject identities; consent and grant; connector manifest and capability version; selected C01–C15 bytes and rights reference; positive and denial fixture; numerical deadline, limit and repetition threshold; evidence owner; expected typed outcome. The later built-artifact manifest records the actual EXE, companion, package, signature, service, client and test-environment hashes before each run. A failed cohort remains visible after repair. Evidence must separate `not_run`, `failed`, `passed_in_source_test`, `passed_installed`, `passed_external_user` and `release_accepted`; none of those states is implied by this document.

The [selected first-cohort compatibility target](eh-g8-cfp1-first-cohort-compatibility-target-v1.md) supplies the **planned** Codex/Windows/game version tuple and explicit drift-denial cases for FC-02 and conditional FC-04. D12 must still freeze the exact public sentence, planned artifact/hardware and each operation threshold; CFP-2/3 then record actual signed bytes and installed compatibility. Do not turn the locally observed version into a support claim without those results.

The [conditional D03 Minecraft freeze](eh-g8-cfp1-conditional-minecraft-shared-action-freeze-v1.md) supplies FC-05/06's exact first action topology, package/adapter/capability identities, one-block effect, finite grant/route clocks and denial matrix. Those rows remain held on R-MC-01, planned-byte/privacy dispositions, D07 validation and D12 wording; a technical freeze is not permission or installed success.
