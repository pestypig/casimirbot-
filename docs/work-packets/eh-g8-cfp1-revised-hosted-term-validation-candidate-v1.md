# CFP-1 revised hosted-term validation candidate v1

## Delegated decision

Use **USD `$60/month` per sponsoring host** as the next D07 validation
candidate. Preserve the provisional offer shape:

- one active or suspended room slot;
- one invited verified guest;
- one connected program;
- P50: up to 50 verified successful owner-granted shared actions per paid term;
- T10: seven days, no card, explicit Start Trial and up to ten verified
  successful shared actions;
- a five-minute deliberately renewed room-activity lease;
- internal PBT plus heterogeneous hard resource ceilings;
- no overage, token pack, wallet, credit balance, top-up, automatic upgrade or
  CasimirBot-funded model/API service.

This revises the assay amount only. It does not accept the action commercially,
freeze public wording or make `$60` a final price. Customers should see the
subscription benefit and successful-action limit. PBT/provider units remain
private nontransferable cost-admission measures, not GPT tokens or purchased
credits.

## Why `$20` was rejected

[D07 integration review 309](../evidence/eh-g8-codex-first-paid-product-delivery-v1/cfp-1/2026-09-21-cfp1-d07-envelope-integration-review-309.md)
confirmed the first rejection case. The later [authorized account read
315](../evidence/eh-g8-codex-first-paid-product-delivery-v1/cfp-1/2026-09-21-cfp1-authorized-account-cost-and-token-direction-315.md)
adds the observed `$20` Replit base-plan assumption. The later identity/signing
guard replaces the earlier `$44.99` pair with `$60.00`, so the intended
ten-paid/five-trial `$20` case is now `−$160.20` partial contribution and a
`−$200.20` residual under the displayed 20%-of-gross sensitivity before other
missing rows. That strengthens, rather than reverses, the prior term packet's
revision trigger.

The next candidate uses the same dated domestic-card planning formula:

```text
fee_net_per_host(60)
  = 60.00 - (0.029 × 60.00 + 0.30) - (0.007 × 60.00)
  = 57.54

included_partial_cost
  = 270.00 common fixed/planning lines
    + hosted PBT reservation
    + 10% of hosted gross
    + 15.00 received-dispute planning case
```

The `$270.00` common line is the selected `$45` Auth0 and `$15` signing cash
ceilings from the [identity/signing guard](eh-g8-cfp1-d07-identity-and-signing-cash-guard-owner-freeze-v1.md),
the observed `$20` Replit base-plan assumption, `$120` routine-support ceiling,
`$60` incident-planning reserve and `$10` distribution reserve. Only the Replit
base amount is account-observed; the two new provider figures are conditional
policy ceilings. Every line retains its stated status, and the combination of
partial costs, assumptions, ceilings and reserves is not a complete cost bound.

Under current partial bounds, the low case requires at least `$112.34` of owner
subsidy to reach zero modeled contribution after the selected baseline reserve,
and `$160.34` to preserve the displayed 20% sensitivity, before remaining
missing rows. Both exceed the existing `$75/month` subsidy cap. The low cohort
is therefore a non-admitted stress case unless the owner separately revises the
subsidy cap, cohort, cost allocation or offer; this amount-only packet does not
revise that cap. The final column subtracts the remaining `$0.25 × Nfree`
reserve because the `$20` base is already in the common line. The intended
cohort therefore has a `$40.40` planning remainder only after the full selected
`$45` baseline/free reserve; that remainder is unallocated exposure, not
margin. Actual provider variable-cash/tax terms, dormant and backup
populations, anonymous distribution traffic, mandatory remedies, qualified
D11 costs and customer-demand evidence remain open and may reject `$60`.

## Decision and implementation boundary

Keep `$60` only while the next completed envelope shows:

1. every material D07 row is exactly `bounded`;
2. T10/P50 remains useful within reviews 306/308's hard ceilings;
3. the intended case remains nonnegative after every accepted cost and the
   final selected contribution/subsidy rule;
4. the low cohort complies with the existing `$75/month` subsidy cap or a
   separately dated owner revision changes the cap, cohort, cost allocation or
   offer, with admission-stop behavior kept explicit;
5. D11 accepts seller, tax, privacy, refund, rights and professional-cost
   conditions; and
6. the product owner makes a dated final D12 selection after seeing the
   completed table.

If any condition fails, revise price, benefit, support model, cohort or provider
architecture rather than hiding cost in customer credits or weakening stop,
revoke, refund, deletion or account remedies. CFP-3 may provision a matching
test Product/Price only after CFP-1 closes and admits the child packet. A live
Price requires later explicit production authorization.
