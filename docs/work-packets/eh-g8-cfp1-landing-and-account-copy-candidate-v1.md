The domain plan calls for a landing page with **Download** and **Open app** routes, while the installed harness supplies free supported personal MCP tools and the hosted service supplies subscribed collaboration. This packet offers **candidate exact sentences and CTA states** for [FC-01–09](eh-g8-cfp1-first-customer-claim-freeze-worksheet-v1.md), based on [owner selection 106](../evidence/eh-g8-codex-first-paid-product-delivery-v1/cfp-1/2026-09-20-cfp1-recommended-direction-owner-selection-106.md). It is not approved public copy. The [operation subcases](eh-g8-cfp1-selected-operation-acceptance-subcases-v1.md) and the actual installed artifact must support every enabled sentence. If an accepted action or connector changes, revise its sentence and cases together before final D12 freeze.

## Pre-release and failure states

Before the relevant gates pass, explanatory pages may say **“CasimirBot’s Windows download and hosted collaboration are in development.”** Render inactive actions with a nearby specific reason, for example **“Download available after signed release”**, **“Trial available when a shared action is ready”** or **“Subscriptions are not open yet.”** These are status messages, not claims of a fixed launch date. A failed Google sign-in, unavailable connector, exhausted provider capacity, missing Price or unverified native effect must display its own truthful recovery or pending state; it must not present a disabled checkbox as a successful connection or silently start a trial. A visitor can still learn about the free personal route without being forced through a paid offer.

If ordinary Google web sign-in works but its sensitive-operation proof or same-ID recovery is not accepted, the affected P1 enrollment or account-change action must say it is unavailable and identify a **reviewed, actually usable** recovery action if one exists. Do not direct a Google-only user to repeat an auto-select sign-in that cannot satisfy freshness, or imply that choosing an account proves reauthentication. Preserve independently valid stop/revoke and truthful account inspection under their separate authority rules. This copy rule does not mark Google web sign-in itself as failed.

For a **future accepted** budget-exhaustion state, use a specific account/room message such as **“Hosted capacity for this term is used. New hosted requests are paused. You can still stop a current action and use personal tools.”** An uncertain native effect needs its own pending/reconciliation message; never describe a held effect slot as verified success or imply that a failed action cost no platform resources. The final message must name the applicable reset, eligible-sponsor handoff or support remedy only after those routes and reviewed terms are proven. These are copy candidates, not current enabled screens or a selected numeric limit.

The first release may omit the personal Minecraft example or developer kit if absent from **all** public implications.
