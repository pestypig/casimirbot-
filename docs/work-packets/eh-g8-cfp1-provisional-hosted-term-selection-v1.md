## Why $20 is the first review candidate

The [room-hour feasibility comparison](../evidence/eh-g8-codex-first-paid-product-delivery-v1/cfp-1/2026-09-20-cfp1-room-hour-feasibility-comparison-152.md) shows the prior `$10` candidate has narrow or negative partial-resource headroom under adverse small-cohort assumptions before identity, signing, support, refund, storage and free-personal allocations. The [signing sensitivity](../evidence/eh-g8-codex-first-paid-product-delivery-v1/cfp-1/2026-09-20-cfp1-small-cohort-signing-cost-sensitivity-261.md) and conditional [Auth0 sensitivity](../evidence/eh-g8-codex-first-paid-product-delivery-v1/cfp-1/2026-09-20-cfp1-auth0-step-up-small-cohort-cost-screen-264.md) make that fixed-cost exposure explicit. The existing `$5` comparator fails more of the same partial screens.

Using the same dated illustrative domestic-card Payments and Billing rates, a `$20` charge leaves **$18.98** before other expenses; ten sponsors leave `$189.80`. After a 20%-of-gross contribution holdback, the adverse 10-paid/one-trial-hour partial resource screen, one deployment base and one modeled database tail per term, the existing arithmetic leaves about **$102.77** before signing, identity, support, refunds, storage, free-personal allocation and the other omitted categories. If both illustrative `$9.99` signing and conditional `$35` identity-plan fixed lines apply, about **$57.78** remains for those omitted categories in that ten-paid/30-trial scenario. These are recombinations of dated sensitivities, not a margin forecast, provider quote or proof that the selected benefit fits.

The later [hard-budget review 306](../evidence/eh-g8-codex-first-paid-product-delivery-v1/cfp-1/2026-09-21-cfp1-d07-t10-p50-hard-budget-independent-review-306.md)
and [platform cost-guard review 308](../evidence/eh-g8-codex-first-paid-product-delivery-v1/cfp-1/2026-09-21-cfp1-d07-platform-cost-guard-independent-review-308.md)
supersede that partial-screen interpretation for the current decision. After
charging the full selected routine-support ceiling and incident reserve, the
intended ten-paid/five-trial case is `−$145.19` before other missing rows and
`−$185.19` under the 20% sensitivity after incorporating the later observed
`$20` Replit base-plan assumption. The `$20` candidate has therefore hit
this packet's revision trigger; it cannot become the final D12 price under the
current assumptions.

The purpose of selecting `$20` is to give D07 one conservative term to validate rather than optimizing around the historical sandbox amount. It does not establish customer willingness to pay, one-subscriber profitability or all-input cost coverage. The price, 50-effect paid target and lease must be revised before D12 if the exact B/P/T route, useful trial, support/refund reserve, selected identity path or qualified review fails the frozen case.

## Validation and return rule

Run the [D07 budget workload](eh-g8-cfp1-d07-hosted-budget-workload-fixture-v1.md) against the selected first-party no-model room/action path. Measure or supply a sourced conservative bound for every worksheet category: shared/free baseline; paid and trial compute, requests, database union/tails, egress and storage; identity; signed delivery; support/reconciliation; processor/refund/dispute; tax treatment; and low-paying/high-trial plus growth/concurrency cases. Test T10/P50 and the 50-effect paid target across intentional visits, unattended tabs, exhaustion, uncertainty, restart and handoff using the [reviewed hard-budget profile](eh-g8-cfp1-d07-t10-p50-usefulness-and-hard-budget-freeze-v1.md). The 10/1 and 20/2 active-hour values remain measurement scenarios, not customer time allowances.

Return this selection to the product owner before D12 when any of these occurs: the 50-effect or T10 promise cannot be reserved under enforceable per-resource bounds; the five-minute lease makes the trial materially unusable; selected fixed plus variable inputs defeat the reviewed contribution floor; account fees materially differ; D11 changes tax/refund/identity treatment; the action lacks rights/technical acceptance; or a wider market is proposed. D12 may publish only the final reviewed price, benefits, limits, seller/market identity and exhaustion/remedy copy. CFP-3 then provisions and verifies a matching test Product/Price and durable meter; this packet changes no account or runtime.
