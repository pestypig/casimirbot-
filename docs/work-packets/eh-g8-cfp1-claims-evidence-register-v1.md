# CFP-1 claims and evidence register v1

The [product goal](../architecture/casimirbot-environment-harness-product-goal-v1.md#product-acceptance-goal) sets twelve obligations for a demonstrated environment harness. The [work program](../helix-environment-harness-work-program-v1.md#active-gate-g8-environment-harness-release-evaluation) alone owns active gate, capability maturity and release authority. The [CFP-1 offer](eh-g8-cfp1-product-rights-and-offer-contract-v1.md#selected-offer-free-personal-tools-paid-hosted-collaboration) chooses free **supported** personal MCP tools, paid hosted collaboration, user-supplied external reasoning and a bounded Minecraft technical pilot. It does not accept a paid product, public user path or any of the twelve G8 outcomes. The older [twelve-scenario proposal](../evidence/eh-g8-codex-first-paid-product-delivery-v1/cfp-1/scope-claims-policy-proposal.md#twelve-canonical-g8-acceptance-scenarios) remains a useful scenario inventory, but its paid-personal assumptions are historical.

## Claim decision rule

A public landing page may describe the intended product and accurately label a feature as **in development**. It may describe an existing alpha/source capability only with its actual access and evidence limits. The Download or Subscribe flow must not imply a signed installer, ordinary-user personal task, paid room, three-person voice mission, model allowance or commercial Minecraft permission until each exact claim passes its owning acceptance and rights gate. A capability in code, a catalog entry, a staged plugin, a developer diagnostic or a checkout preset is not a customer result. CFP-1 freezes the proposed wording and proof obligation; CFP-5/6 decide external-user value and release claims after the implementation stages.

The concise architectural description in the product goal may be used in planning with a clear development qualifier. The separate Helix parity and canonical G8 requirements remain visible even if the first paid collaboration SKU is deliberately bounded.

## Acceptance handoff

CFP-1.SCOPE/CLAIMS owns the wording/evidence map and freezes each selected pilot and launch claim **before** evaluation; the product owner accepts external copy. CFP-2 proves the first free personal task and exact client surface. CFP-3 proves hosted SKU, rights-compliant distribution and signed release machinery. CFP-4 repeats identity, entitlement, collaboration, recovery and G8 slices on one declared artifact. CFP-5 obtains external-user value/support evidence. CFP-6 conducts claim-by-claim release review. The work program alone may advance maturity or stages. Every failed or not-run scenario remains visible; no narrower pilot quietly deletes a product-goal obligation.
