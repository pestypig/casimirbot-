# CFP-1 hosted resource-budget token contract v1

The owner suggested [resource tokens as a closer expense measure than time](../evidence/eh-g8-codex-first-paid-product-delivery-v1/cfp-1/2026-09-20-cfp1-owner-resource-token-direction-154.md) and [reaffirmed budgeting against expenses as the user base grows](../evidence/eh-g8-codex-first-paid-product-delivery-v1/cfp-1/2026-09-20-cfp1-owner-resource-budget-scale-direction-169.md). Prefer an internal resource budget over a customer-facing active-room-hour allowance **if the measured or conservatively bounded cost case supports it**. Use a **recurring subscription for access to hosted collaboration** and model its variable service load internally as nontransferable `platform_budget_tokens` (PBT). PBT are not LLM input/output tokens, Stripe credits, a customer wallet, a transferable balance, or a way to buy native effects. CasimirBot does not meter the customer's own Codex subscription or fund inference in the selected initial offer. A guest consumes the sponsoring host's eligible hosted capacity, while their account, consent and any program grant remain separate. Free personal MCP access and stop/revoke do not debit hosted PBT; their real operating cost remains in baseline B and the shared-cost allocation.

## Accounting model to test before D07

Use **USD $0.001 of modeled attributable marginal platform cost per PBT** as a provisional *internal accounting denomination*, with integer arithmetic and a versioned conversion table. This denomination is not a public price and may be revised before final terms. For each sponsor term, record provider raw units and money separately from tokens and actual cash due:

```text
modeled_variable_usd = Σ(resource_quantity_i × dated_rate_i)
                    + directly attributable identity/transport/delivery cost
platform_budget_tokens = ceil(modeled_variable_usd / 0.001 USD)
```

Apply that ceiling to the **cumulative modeled variable cost of one sponsor term**, not once per request, effect, room lease or resource line. Preserve a higher-precision fixed-point money accumulator at the provider's documented meter/rate precision; the displayed integer PBT debit is `ceil(cumulative_settled_cost / denomination)`, and an incremental settlement changes it by the difference from the previous cumulative debit. Ten $0.0001 operations therefore settle to **one PBT** in total, not ten. Room recreation cannot reset the sponsor-term accumulator. A handoff starts a different eligible sponsor term and keeps the predecessor's accumulated cost and causal liabilities attributable to that origin. Pin both the modeled provider-rate table/conversion version and allowance for an active term; record any actual midterm provider-rate increase in a separate gross-cost and operator-exposure reconciliation, not as a retroactive PBT debit. A reviewed new model applies to future terms unless the accepted customer terms explicitly permit an in-term change. The deployment-wide spend guard and resource ceilings still protect the operator when actual rates change.

The summation includes Autoscale CPU/memory compute units, requests and outbound bytes; production-database active time, idle tails and storage growth; and any separately metered identity, room transport, media, update delivery or connector service used by the selected benefit. Distinguish gross resource use, included Replit allowance/prepayment and cash due. Allocate shared active time and concurrency using a stated, testable rule; do not assign the whole database or deployment bill to every overlapping host, and do not assign unobserved resources zero. Keep the source, date, currency, effective rate, units, uncertainty, provider invoice/usage reference and model version in access-controlled financial evidence. Public CFP-1 summaries contain no account-private rates or derived amounts.

The [completed Replit period](../evidence/eh-g8-codex-first-paid-product-delivery-v1/cfp-1/2026-09-20-cfp1-completed-replit-period-cost-source-173.md) supplies historical project resource units and invoice arithmetic for calibrating a later conversion table. The later [authorized account read](../evidence/eh-g8-codex-first-paid-product-delivery-v1/cfp-1/2026-09-21-cfp1-authorized-account-cost-and-token-direction-315.md) establishes a dated `$20/month` Replit Core base-plan assumption and confirms that variable AI, hosting, traffic/data, storage and database usage remain separate. Neither read contains matched sponsor, room or trial counters or a provider-enforced whole-account hard cap, so they cannot determine a per-term PBT allowance or show that the provisional denomination covers the selected workload. Use gross resource rates for modeled consumption, then reconcile the base plan, included allowance, pre-purchase, discounts and tax separately in the financial case.

The [D07 provider-unit assay](eh-g8-cfp1-d07-provider-unit-allocation-and-assay-v1.md) is the acceptance rule for that reconciliation: background, sponsor-attributed and unattributed project units must partition without overlap; a shared database idle tail cannot be billed to every concurrent sponsor; and an included/pre-purchased plan allowance cannot be counted again as independent cash expense. If only aggregate provider usage is available, keep a bounded cohort estimate and conservative admission reservation rather than reporting precise per-sponsor cost.

PBT cover **meterable variable cost**, not the whole subscription cost. The D07 price calculation must separately reserve allocated base-plan/service costs, support and incident time, expected trial starts including nonconverters, payment/refund/dispute costs, nonrecoverable tax and free-personal cross-subsidy. A paid or trial token ceiling is therefore chosen only after the [unit-economics worksheet](eh-g8-cfp1-hosted-unit-economics-worksheet-v1.md) shows the intended, lower-paying-host and higher-trial-start cases. The budget model must include the [first-party domain guest request](eh-g8-cfp1-hosted-guest-request-ingress-v1.md), identity/session work, invite, owner offer/pre-grant creation, guest request, native action/result, history and return visits as well as room idle presence; an effect count alone is not a cost proxy. A guest-owned MCP-only measurement cannot establish the no-model-account browser path's cost. The 20/2 and 10/1 room-hour cases in the [pilot assay](eh-g8-cfp1-hosted-pilot-price-and-capacity-assay-v1.md) become **load-sampling scenarios**, not customer time allowances.

The subscriber allowance is a **per-sponsor admission control**, not a forecast of the whole platform bill. For each candidate user-base size, cost free personal users, trial starts, paying sponsors and peak simultaneous rooms together. Recalculate shared database active time, Autoscale contention or tier changes, identity, storage, support and payment costs at that concurrency; do not multiply one isolated-host marginal cost by the user count and assume the result remains valid. Record the cohort's contribution after shared/fixed allocation, its worst supported burst and the deployment-wide spending guard separately from each sponsor's remaining PBT. If a growth threshold changes provider rates or capacity, choose a new prospective budget/price version for future terms after review; never silently debit an active customer's allowance because another customer joined.

## Admission and settlement contract

Before accepting each chargeable hosted operation, reserve a conservative PBT upper bound for its finite CPU/wall deadline, request count, response bytes, database work and any independently metered connector path. A connected room may incur costs between explicit actions; measure that state and renew only short, finite room leases against available capacity if needed. Enforce separate hard ceilings for request frequency, concurrency, bytes, operation duration, stored history and a global deployment spend/capacity safety budget. A scalar token total cannot replace those per-resource bounds: delayed provider metering, an unbounded request or a long-lived connection could overshoot before reconciliation. On exhaustion, deny or suspend **new** hosted work with an inspectable reason and recovery path; preserve owner stop/revoke, safe native release, term inspection and the free personal path. The [authorized Replit control read](../evidence/eh-g8-codex-first-paid-product-delivery-v1/cfp-1/2026-09-21-cfp1-replit-monthly-budget-control-read-317.md) proves an account-level additional-usage budget exists and provider exhaustion suspends services broadly. [Scope read 321](../evidence/eh-g8-codex-first-paid-product-delivery-v1/cfp-1/2026-09-21-cfp1-replit-account-wide-budget-scope-321.md) shows the current control covers `All workspaces` and both Resource and AI categories. The [selected provider route](eh-g8-cfp1-d07-provider-isolation-and-budget-mapping-v1.md) requires a dedicated production billing account; the [numeric guard](eh-g8-cfp1-d07-numeric-provider-cash-guard-owner-freeze-v1.md) selects a `$100` provider shutdown and `$75` internal pause under the `$200` whole-project ceiling. These are deployment safeguards, not sponsor entitlement. If CFP-3 cannot prove isolation, the exact plan/tax/rates, or the earlier internal pause, return D07 rather than debit unrelated use, raise a limit or silently admit against the shared account.

The [D07 finite-lease sensitivity](eh-g8-cfp1-d07-provider-unit-allocation-and-assay-v1.md) and [T10/P10 workload fixture](eh-g8-cfp1-d07-hosted-budget-workload-fixture-v1.md) are admission fixtures for this ledger. Reserve the candidate lease's maximum request, compute, egress and room-caused database-active interval **including its idle tail** before allowing an intentional visit; record its identity and return proven excess only after reconciliation. If a sponsor makes thirty short visits but only ten yield verified effects, the cost ledger still accounts for all thirty admitted visits while the effect ledger counts ten. A suspended room occupies the provisional trial room slot but does not automatically renew its active lease or reset the sponsor's PBT; if the next reservation cannot fit, keep the page suspended and let the owner inspect or stop it. These are workload tests, not chosen customer limits or a substitute for a deployment-wide cohort budget.

Reserve against the **same cumulative term balance**: atomically calculate `ceil((settled_cost + all_pending_upper_bounds + new_upper_bound) / denomination)` and compare it with the term allowance before dispatch. A new reservation may add zero whole PBT when earlier reservations already cover the next fractional increment, but it still needs a unique work/lease identity, a recorded upper-bound amount and the independent per-resource ceilings. Include the provider's conservative **post-close causal database idle tail** and delayed network/storage usage in the reservation for the room lease or final work that can trigger them. Room close, expiry and host handoff stop new origin-term admissions but keep those origin liabilities held and reconcilable; do not reset, transfer to the successor or release them merely because the room interval ended. Settlement replaces a reservation with measured cost or a conservative retained upper bound, recomputes the cumulative debit and releases only proven excess; an uncertain operation or delayed causal liability keeps its upper bound until reconciliation. Serialize concurrent reservation, settlement and term-revision changes across instances. Do not independently round and sum each operation's token charge, let parallel requests each reuse the same available fractional headroom, or turn a rounding-zero request into unlimited free work.

The [current route/limiter source audit](../evidence/eh-g8-codex-first-paid-product-delivery-v1/cfp-1/2026-09-20-cfp1-hosted-rate-limit-versus-cost-budget-source-audit-193.md) finds process-local IP/principal and connector-authority throttles plus request-body ceilings. They are useful abuse/transport bounds but carry no durable sponsor-term PBT state and cannot set the paid/trial allowance. CFP-3 must prove cost admission across two instances and a restart; a rate-limiter pass or reset never replenishes a sponsor term.

The [provider-spend source boundary](../evidence/eh-g8-codex-first-paid-product-delivery-v1/cfp-1/2026-09-20-cfp1-hosted-model-spend-source-boundary-199.md) separately finds that current Shared GPT Live Room admission and SDP exchange are tied to model/voice consent and an OpenAI API key path. The selected first-offer [room seam](eh-g8-cfp1-hosted-provider-spend-and-room-seam-v1.md) must prove the hosted trial/paid shared-action journey without that provider path. Existing account-policy `model_tokens_per_day` is a model-selection quota, not sponsor-term PBT; no model token count can be substituted for the raw hosting meters above. If the first offer's route invokes a platform-funded provider, D07 must reject the zero-provider-cost assumption and the product owner must reselect and cost the benefit before customer claims.

Settle reservations from attributable measured units or a conservative upper bound when provider detail is delayed, with one durable sponsor-term revision and room-lineage key. Replays and duplicate effect requests must never cause a second native effect or double-settle the same work identity; genuinely repeated network work may still consume platform resources and requires a separate abuse/rate guard. Failed, denied and uncertain requests may cost PBT even though they consume **no verified-successful-effect quota**. Hold uncertain native effect capacity until postcondition or proven no-effect reconciliation under the separate T3 rule. An accounting adjustment cannot manufacture a successful native effect. Audit pre-reserved, settled, released and unattributed PBT; reject an unexplained negative balance or silently unpriced resource category. Reconcile aggregate ledger units to provider project usage and B/P/T windows, with privacy-reviewed retention.

Host handoff closes the former sponsor's admission interval and starts a new interval under the successor's own eligible paid/trial term. No PBT, trial days, program grant or model account transfers. In-flight work, later-billed causal idle time and delayed network/storage usage stay attributed to the origin term and are fenced by the separate sponsor/owner/grant revisions; keep a closed term reconcilable without reviving its admission authority. Room recreation and a second device must not reset one sponsor's usage. The seven-day trial calendar clock remains seven continuous days; any final PBT limit is additional to the owner's provisional one room, one guest, one connected program and ten **verified** effect targets. The paid term remains recurring, with no automatic usage overage charge, token pack, credit posting or replenishment other than the frozen term transition. If the token ceiling cannot let a representative guest use the promised trial action ten times, revise the cost model, price or benefit before release; do not advertise an unusable trial.

Include delayed A-attributed network/storage usage after handoff. A separate rate fixture pins A's active-term model at `r1`, records a later actual provider rate `r2` and operator variance, and applies any reviewed `r2` conversion only to future terms. These fixtures validate ledger arithmetic and attribution only; they are not B/P/T cost measurements or selected customer allowances.
