## Frozen replay

The admission implementation imports neither the projection producer nor
SciPy. It reads only the fixed global-center and projected-center paths. It
rejects symlinks and any inventory other than the five payloads plus
`receipt.json`.

The replay independently performs:

1. canonical JSON and length-delimited self-hash checks for both receipts;
2. exact payload size and SHA-256 checks in ordinal order;
3. little-endian binary64 decoding with finite and negative-zero rejection;
4. exact comparison of the nine scalar words to values recomputed from the
   global-center receipt;
5. the even origin recurrence through index 16 below `x=2^-12`;
6. a direct scalar cubic-Hermite formula over the stored mesh through `x=32`,
   without SciPy;
7. the frozen exponential/power and `-C/x` exterior extension above `x=32`;
8. direct Chebyshev evaluation of both 128-word core coefficient payloads at
   the 128 frozen compactified Lobatto nodes, both endpoints, and the join;
9. exact positive-zero validation of all 64 tail words.

The independent node screen is `2^-40` relative to `1+abs(value)` and the join
screen is `2^-28` absolute. Endpoint reconstruction is screened by `2^-40`.
The primary diagnostic numbers are retained only as bound observations; they
do not decide admission.

The sole output is
`artifacts/nhm2-spherical-boson-star-v2-g2/lambda-zero-proof-center-admission-v1.json`.
It must be absent before the one authorized materialization. Any failure is
terminal for this admission packet and cannot select a new center, codec,
interpolant, node count, or tolerance.

## Authority boundary

An admitted proof center is still a binary64 approximation proposed as the
center of a later directed enclosure. The admission receipt proves only that
the frozen bytes are internally bound and independently reconstruct the same
preregistered representation within the frozen screens. Ground-state
acceptance, proof completion, candidate admission, replay authority,
diagnostic-lamp authority, physical viability, propulsion, and transport all
remain false.
