## Distinguishability decision

One complete P8N representative receipt would be evidence-distinguishable. It
could show concentration by global degree and origin envelope, a broad or
unseparated distribution, exact P8I/P8N binding failure, or a bounded timeout.
The scientific question is therefore meaningful.

However, the present execution is not sufficiently turnaround-bounded to
freeze a responsible one-shot proposal:

- authenticated P8J processed 65,536 panels on 32 threads in 6,044 seconds;
- P8N first reruns that complete P8I selector and then enters a separate serial
  65,536-panel observer loop;
- the representative coverage is 16,638 direct terms per panel, or exactly
  1,090,387,968 term observations in that serial phase;
- scaling the authenticated 32-thread selector wall time to equivalent serial
  panel work gives 53.724444 hours before accounting for P8M's additional six-
  envelope bookkeeping. This is a workload comparison, not a completion ETA;
- P8N's only runtime evidence is a two-panel, lower-order manufactured fixture
  with 2,172 observed terms; and
- the frozen P8N interface exposes no observer-phase progress callback. A
  timeout could therefore preserve liveness evidence but no bounded scientific
  panel prefix from the long serial phase.

A broad multi-day timeout would cap cost but would not satisfy the packet's
requirement for a defensible turnaround analysis and meaningful complete or
partial evidence. The direct representative proposal is therefore not frozen.
This is an execution-readiness stop, not a scientific failure and not evidence
against the term/radius diagnostic.
