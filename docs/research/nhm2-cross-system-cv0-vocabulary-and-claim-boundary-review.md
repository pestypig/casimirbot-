## Purpose

Freeze the scientific language and causal boundaries required to compare
ordinary gravity, stellar and material support, bosonic phases, boson stars,
Casimir sources, quantum coherence, and proposed state reduction without
silently treating them as one mechanism.

The resolution backbone is:

\[
\boxed{
\text{prepared source/state}
\rightarrow
\text{stress-energy and fluctuations}
\rightarrow
\text{support and competing rates}
\rightarrow
\text{geometry and dynamics}
\rightarrow
\text{observable}
\rightarrow
\text{model discrimination}.
}
\]

Sharing this backbone transfers an interface. It does not transfer a physical
conclusion.

acceptance rule, it is out of scope and must return to the controlling program

## Transition map

Permitted phase path:

> thermal/noncondensed gas
> \(\rightarrow\) condensation transition or finite-size crossover
> \(\rightarrow\) BEC
> \(\rightarrow\) authenticated superfluid response
> \(\rightarrow\) supersolid after additional density order
> \(\rightarrow\) vortex-bearing supersolid after authenticated defect nucleation.

An incoherent droplet crystal is a competing branch, not an automatic
supersolid stage.

Permitted gravitational path:

> stable/support-dominated configuration
> \(\rightarrow\) registered growing mode
> \(\rightarrow\) nonlinear evolution
> \(\rightarrow\) relaxation, fragmentation, dispersion, equilibrium
> migration, or horizon formation.

Permitted quantum-state path:

> prepared state
> \(\rightarrow\) demonstrated coherence
> \(\rightarrow\) distinguishable mass-density branches
> \(\rightarrow\) measured coherence evolution
> \(\rightarrow\) comparison of environmental, unitary, and registered
> reduction models.

No arrow may jump from one path to another without a typed bridge, source
identity, observable, and falsifier.

## Theory Badge Graph edge-intent grammar

The phrases in the tables below are human-readable semantic intents for a
future graph proposal. They are **not** values of `TheoryBadgeEdgeV1.relation`
and this packet does not register an edge. A later CV graph packet must select
one of the canonical schema relations (`derives`, `requires`, `specializes`,
`approximates`, `bounds`, `shares_units`, `uses_constant`,
`numerically_solves`, `diagnostic_checks`, `documents`, or `blocks`) and must
place the more specific phrase in `label` and `claimBoundaryNote`.

The default mapping discipline is:

- benchmark calibration may propose `diagnostic_checks`;
- a genuine input dependency or causal mediator may propose `requires`;
- a controlled limiting relation may propose `approximates`;
- a hard falsifier or authority barrier may propose `blocks`;
- an analogy, historical link, or non-transfer statement may propose
  `documents`; and
- a resolution outcome belongs in an authenticated receipt, not in an edge
  relation.

None of those mappings is automatic. Badge identity, observable identity,
units, frame, domain, error contract, evidence authority, and independent
promotion review remain mandatory under the canonical graph contract.

- condensate coherence does not establish mass-branch superposition;
- coherence does not establish entanglement;
- entanglement does not establish metric connectivity;
- laboratory phase calibrates method, not self-gravity;
- vortex dynamics does not establish gravitational collapse;
- Casimir-force validation does not establish Casimir-sourced curvature;

Every future edge must carry source evidence IDs/hashes, maturity, domain,
maximum claim, invalidation dependencies, and provenance. An edge label alone

## Resolution-outcome grammar

| Outcome | Minimum evidence | Maximum claim |
|---|---|---|
| Integrity failure | A required identity, provenance, chronology, or hard integrity duty fails | No physical conclusion |
| Null-consistent | The frozen analysis is consistent with the null at achieved sensitivity | Apparatus-specific upper bounds or no detected departure |
| Environment-explained residual | A registered ordinary coupling quantitatively accounts for the residual | Quantified ordinary explanation in the tested apparatus/domain |
| Unexplained residual | Residual survives registered controls without unique discrimination | Anomaly only; replication and expanded systematics required |
| Model-discriminating residual | A preregistered model wins the frozen comparison and required independent replication closes | Conditional evidence favoring only that registered model/domain |
| Known physics recovered | Frozen calibration and held-out agreement | Recovery in tested domain |
| Candidate falsified | Immutable first hard failure under frozen rules | Candidate rejected in registered domain |
| Bounded exclusion | Powered sensitivity and nuisance-marginalized exclusion | Only frozen region excluded |
| Models not identifiable | Registered alternatives overlap at achieved sensitivity | No preference |
| Source-to-observable closure | Full authenticated causal chain and replay duties close | Closure for exact model/domain |

The first five labels are preserved exactly from the Casimir-DP experiment
proposal. Elsewhere in this packet, `model favored` is a generic explanatory
term only; a Casimir-DP result must emit `model-discriminating residual` and
must satisfy that proposal's preregistration and independent-replication rule.

Nulls, exclusions, falsifications, and identified degeneracies are successful
resolutions when they satisfy their contracts. A pursuit must not remain open
solely because it did not yield a preferred positive result.
