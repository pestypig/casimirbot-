# The cesium correction is directly contested

The prior neutral-current screen used a February preprint as an explicitly unverified alternative theory baseline. Further primary-source inspection changes its status: the claim is now published, directly challenged, and defended in the published response. It remains unsuitable as an automatic adjustment to our constraint calculation.

## Authenticated sources

- **Flambaum and Samsonov:** [Effects of dispersion parity-violating interaction in electron scattering and atoms](https://journals.aps.org/prd/pdf/10.1103/cb8l-bt9n), Physical Review D 114, L011302, published July 6, 2026. This is the journal successor to arXiv:2602.22466. The percent-level cesium correction remains. Its page-four note responds explicitly to the finite-size criticism: the authors identify their contribution with short-distance electron-quark matching, followed by nuclear matrix elements, and reject using the coherent elastic nuclear form factor to cut off that matching.
- **Gorchtein and Spiesberger:** [Parity violation in atoms: neutrino-mediated long range forces and finite nuclear size](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.17059), retrieved PDF marked arXiv:2605.17059v2, May 26, 2026, with internal date May 27. They incorporate a dispersive nuclear form factor and obtain cancellation of the space-integrated coherent potential, leaving a very small atomic-scale effect. Their calculation directly disputes the sizable atomic interpretation. The [HTML rendering](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.17059v2) has a different displayed internal date, so the PDF's version stamp is used here.

These are opposing primary analyses, not two independent measurements. Publication of the first is stronger provenance than the previously inspected preprint; it does not settle the calculation or invalidate the critique by itself. Conversely, the critique's small coherent contribution does not, by itself, demonstrate that every hard local coefficient vanishes.

## What must be compared

Our own organizing argument is to write the low-energy electron-nucleus amplitude schematically as

`A(Q) = sum_i C_i(mu) <N|O_i(mu)|N> + A_nonlocal(Q;mu)`.

The coefficients C_i contain short-distance matching. Nuclear form factors belong in the appropriate matrix elements; nonlocal contributions must be defined with the same subtractions. Scale or scheme dependence can cancel only in the combined amplitude. This equation is a bookkeeping framework, not a completed derivation of either paper's coefficient.

There is a simple distinction that does not require choosing a side. If a local coefficient C multiplies a normalized nuclear density rho, its spatial potential is C rho(r) and its integral is C, because integral rho=1. Equivalently, multiplication by a form factor with F(0)=1 preserves that constant zero-momentum amplitude. Nuclear smearing can change the profile without deleting an already matched contact coefficient.

Separately, a nonanalytic term proportional to Q^2 log(Q^2/mu^2) approaches zero as Q approaches zero. A claim about this term or its coherent potential alone cannot determine an independent constant in the full amplitude. A low-energy discontinuity does not fix arbitrary polynomial subtraction terms. This observation does not prove that the proposed extra constant is NEW: it may overlap a conventional coefficient already included in the SM weak-charge reference.

The decisive missing calculation is therefore a comparison, in one renormalization and electroweak-input scheme, of the proposed diagrams with the existing weak-charge coefficients. It must include counterterms and input conversions, identify the already included pieces, and combine the remainder with nuclear response once. Neither adopting a short-distance cutoff nor removing it, without this comparison, answers the overlap question.

## Consequence for our shared model

The alternate +0.58608 cesium central-value shift in the [prior screen](casimir-dp-axion-neutral-current-screen-2026-09-07.md) remains a historical sensitivity exercise. It is not an admitted correction, an uncertainty interval, or a reason to favor a different yL. That packet is preserved as an evidence snapshot; this note supplies the updated literature status.

Keep the original messenger reference and report APV constraints conditional on their declared theory extraction. Do not average the two theoretical claims, combine their central values as independent data, or claim that the disagreement establishes a new coherence mechanism. A conservative static interaction and a predicted loss of center-of-mass coherence require different observables; an atomic contact correction supplies no scattering/decoherence rate for the canonical apparatus on its own.

For continued progress, this disputed APV interpretation should not become a prerequisite for every other calculation. The next independent route is the messenger's flavor embedding and associated rare-process constraints. A full electroweak matching audit remains necessary before claiming precision-level admission, but the frozen two-target tree reference can continue to be assessed conditionally.
