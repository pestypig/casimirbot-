# Conditional virtual-electron spin contribution

Exploratory component calculation, September 7, 2026. No parameter retuning or complete model admission.

Take the explicitly conditional leading operator B*(sigma_chi dot sigma_e), with B=-1.23584836e-12 GeV^-2, from the preceding vector-pair calculation. In the spin-degenerate independent-orbital approximation, each Pauli-spin response equals the independent density response and mixed density/spin terms vanish. This uses the unscreened independent response recovered from Im(epsilon_noLFE), not the screened loss function. The earlier authenticated spin-bubble packet supplies that response and records its limitations.

Its reference photon spin amplitude is (2*e*mu_gamma/m_e)*S_chi dot P_transverse dot S_e, whose free-spin average squared is e^2*mu_gamma^2/(2*m_e^2). The isotropic contact amplitude has average squared 3*B^2. Because the independent spin response is proportional to the identity in spin-index space, their integrated rates have the constant ratio 6*m_e^2*B^2/(e^2*mu_gamma^2). Explicit Pauli matrices in the script check both factors; no photon nuclear rate is reused for the virtual model.

At the reference moment 1e-6 GeV^-1, this ratio is 2.60943464e-17. Applying it to the 100 GeV response gives D_spin,covered <= 1.65683296e-42. The inequality is inherited from the branch factor bounded by two, not an experimental upper limit. The grid covers 5.5-150 eV and archived momentum centers about 37 eV/c to 74.6 keV/c. Numerical Simpson/trapezoid difference is inherited at 0.1314 percent; it is not a physical uncertainty bound.

This is a constant-coefficient independent-spin approximation. It does not validate the conditional Majorana amplitude, nonforward terms, interacting/excitonic spin response, spin-orbit effects, defects, subgap states, finite surfaces or surviving-event visibility. Its grid differs from the recent density component, and no sum is presented as a full material prediction. The microscopic-response distinction is discussed in the [multi-channel framework](https://arxiv.org/html/1910.08092); the archived independent spin construction is defined in the earlier local spin-bubble packet.

Decision: this resolved approximation does not make the virtual benchmark a promising measurable-overlap lead. Further refinement of its tiny density and independent-spin components is lower priority than a substantive correction to matching or an explicit additional physical mechanism. The full shared model remains unestablished; the research goal stays active.
