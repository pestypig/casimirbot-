## Search and evidence admission

Positive scores require primary sources: original papers/preprints reporting the authors' own theorem, construction, computation, convergence study, or machine-readable data. Reviews may locate sources but cannot raise a score. Each claim must bind a stable URL/identifier and a precise passage, equation, table, or figure. Inaccessible or family-adjacent evidence is unresolved and scored down.

For every identity, the bounded search must include at least:

- one global classical/domain/regularity query;
- one stability, spectral-positivity, or vacuum-limit query;
- one same-family Hadamard/RSET/noise query; and
- one independent implementation, convergence, or replay query.

Search absence means `not found`, never nonexistence.

- `S=2`: the source-defined accepted member is genuinely static and its spacetime metric is one global `C∞` Lorentzian metric;

## Ranking and stop rule

For each eligible row:

```text
minimum = min(S,D,L,V,K,Q,P,B,R,F)
total   = S+D+L+V+K+Q+P+B+R+F
rank    = (Q, minimum, total, P, K, B, R, F, S, D, L, V)
```

Ranks compare lexicographically, larger first. `Q` leads because the benchmark exists to validate the complete semiclassical chain, not merely another classical star solver.

Select only if exactly one eligible identity has the maximum rank and its member rule fixes one source-tabulated member or a unique pre-result source-coordinate rule. No eligible row, any top-rank tie, missing member identity, or protocol-integrity failure returns `NO_SELECTION`. Candidate IDs are not tie-breakers.
