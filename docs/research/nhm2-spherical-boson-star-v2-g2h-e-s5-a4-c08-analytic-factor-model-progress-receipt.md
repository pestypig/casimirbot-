## Decision

```text
F(t)  = 1 - 2*mu*t
E1(t) = exp(2*mu*t)
E2(t) = (1 + 2*mu*t)*exp(2*mu*t)
```

positive-panel order and carries the complete value, three first, and nine

exact left-centered exponential coefficient recurrence, and `E2` by multiplying
that series by its exact degree-one factor. For each exponential factor and
jet, the complete function is interval-evaluated over the whole panel, the
retained polynomial is interval-Horner evaluated over the same panel, and the
directed upper magnitude of their difference is stored as a symmetric
remainder. No sampled node or midpoint selection participates.
