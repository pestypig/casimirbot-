## Stop rule

Any G2F hard falsifier produces a typed G2G stop. It does not authorize changing
`mu`, `beta`, matter family, quantum state, chart, tolerances or candidate identity.
If the exact member cannot be completely defined, G2G closes without a candidate
and returns to an explicitly authorized research gate; it does not select a nearby
member after observing calculations.

## Authority locks

No evaluator, solver, quantum mode computation or output root is authorized by
this packet. Candidate admission, classical proof, joint geometry/state,
readiness, lanes, replay, diagnostic lamp, physical viability, propulsion and
transport remain false.
