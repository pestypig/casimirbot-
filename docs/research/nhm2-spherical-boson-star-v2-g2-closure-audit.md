## Definition closure that is absent

The vacuum ABI has **44 exact null choices**. They fall into six causal groups:

1. **Equation and representation:** desingularized `G`, its source binding,
   packed unknown vector, spatial/parameter Chebyshev bases and maps, parameter
   construction chronology, and coefficient codec.
2. **Banach-space and tail closure:** coefficient norm and component weights,
   analytic tail factorization, core-tail join, and finite/infinite split.
3. **Validated operator:** approximate derivative and inverse, radii-bound
   assembly, interval Newton predicate, and exact `Y`, `Z0`, `Z1`, `Z2` rules.
4. **Cover topology:** singularity separation, tube definition, shared-face
   overlap, compatible orientation, and the λ=0 limiting ground-state, simple
   kernel, transversality, and first-tube containment definitions.
5. **Terminal binding:** terminal state definition/source, lift, containment
   predicate, and exact bounded input-manifest inventory/count.
6. **Execution authority:** interval dependency lock, proof source manifest,
   toolchain, executable, runtime, issuer, builder, command, and proof preseal.

The ABI requires all 44 before verifier implementation or traversal. A method
label, sealed null field, synthetic fixture, seven finite continuation stages,
or the N=64 result cannot fill any of them.

## Reuse and non-reuse boundary

The existing v3 Newton algebra, spectral construction, corrected LU solve,
frozen thresholds, and result encoding are reusable design evidence. Reuse
requires a new preregistered source closure because v3 hard-codes N=64 and one
input chronology.

The cross-grid and boundary kernels may be reused only as calculation kernels
behind authenticated input/runtime/issuer wrappers. Their current receipts are
not proof receipts and must not be promoted or rewritten.

The primary and source-disjoint replay share the same MPFR/GMP runtime lineage.
They remain useful agreement evidence but do not satisfy runtime-disjoint
independent replay.
