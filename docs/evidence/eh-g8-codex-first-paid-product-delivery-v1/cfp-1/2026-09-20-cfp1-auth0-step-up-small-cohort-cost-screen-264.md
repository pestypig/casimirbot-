[Auth0's displayed B2C pricing](https://auth0.com/pricing), checked 2026-09-20, lists Free at $0/month with passkeys, and Essentials at **$35/month** for the displayed 500-monthly-active-user selection with Pro Multi-Factor Authentication. Its comparison marks Pro MFA Factors unavailable on Free. [Auth0's WebAuthn MFA documentation](https://auth0.com/docs/secure/multi-factor-authentication/fido-authentication-with-webauthn/configure-webauthn-security-keys-for-mfa) says feature availability varies by plan and login implementation. This does **not** show that every Auth0 passkey flow is a qualifying operation-time MFA step-up, that the owner's tenant is on Free or Essentials, or that any factor can be enrolled/recovered for the selected Google-only user. The actual tenant, agreement, factor, challenge trace, capacity, regional pricing and tax must be checked before D02 chooses this route.

The [small-cohort signing screen](2026-09-20-cfp1-small-cohort-signing-cost-sensitivity-261.md) models one $10 monthly domestic-card subscription leaving **$9.34** after [Stripe's standard domestic-card Payments rate](https://stripe.com/pricing) and [Billing pay-as-you-go rate](https://stripe.com/billing/pricing), plus one illustrative **$9.99/month** [Microsoft Artifact Signing Basic account](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/artifact-signing/how-to-change-sku). If the chosen P1 step-up actually requires an additional Auth0 Essentials subscription at the displayed $35/month, the two illustrative fixed lines total **$44.99/month**. At $10, **five** fee-net paying sponsors are needed merely to exceed those two fixed lines; at a hypothetical $5/month, **ten** are needed. With ten paying sponsors, the $10 comparator leaves **$48.41**, and the $5 comparator leaves **$0.21**, before Replit hosting/database, identity-related usage, free-personal service, trial starts, support, refunds, disputes, tax and any desired contribution. The arithmetic uses fee-net `N × $9.34` or `N × $4.52` minus `$44.99`; actual per-payment rounding and provider/account terms may differ.

This is **not** a claim that CasimirBot must buy Essentials or that a free passkey path is security-equivalent. It makes the dependency explicit: D02 must verify a reachable, independently reviewed fresh step-up and recovery path; D07 must include the selected provider's actual fixed and variable charges in the same B/P/T and low-paying/high-trial case. A free-tier route may lower a fixed cost only if its tenant-configured factor, challenge identity, operation-time proof, enrollment and recovery all pass the D02 contract. A paid tier cannot be chosen by ignoring its small-cohort cost, and a cheaper route cannot be chosen by weakening identity proof. No Auth0, Azure, Stripe, Replit, account, runtime or production setting changed.
