# CFP-1 D06 provisional refund calculation review — 2026-09-20

Program gate: **G8 — Environment-harness release evaluation**. This is a technical/source specification review of the owner's [provisional D06 selection](2026-09-20-cfp1-recommended-direction-owner-selection-106.md), not a qualified financial/tax return, live Stripe configuration, payment or customer term approval.

The [paid-host deletion packet](../../../work-packets/eh-g8-cfp1-paid-host-account-deletion-term-decision-v1.md#provisional-unused-time-payment-refund-calculation-for-d11-review) now fixes the selected basis into integer minor-unit arithmetic: the settled current-period subscription base after discounts, excluding separately identified pass-through tax; actual authoritative paid-period seconds; one durable deletion-fence timestamp retained through retries; customer-favoring ceiling; and subtraction of already confirmed same-charge base refunds. Worked no-tax examples return $6.67 for a $10/30-day charge deleted at day 10, $5.67 more after a confirmed earlier $1 same-charge refund, $4.00 for an $8 discounted charge deleted halfway, and $0 after term end. A local arithmetic check reproduced 667/567/400/0 minor units. [Stripe's cancellation guide](https://docs.stripe.com/billing/subscriptions/cancel), [refund guide](https://docs.stripe.com/refunds) and [refund API](https://docs.stripe.com/api/refunds/create) were rechecked 2026-09-20; cancellation, credit and payment refund remain distinct processor operations.

The first independent read found a **material pending-refund race**: subtracting only confirmed refunds could issue the full deletion refund while a separate pending same-charge refund later succeeds. The packet now holds new automatic deletion-refund submission whenever another same-charge refund is nonterminal, preserves the original deletion time, serializes app-origin refund requests, re-reads processor state, and recomputes after success/failure/cancellation. The $1 pending example and CFP-3 sandbox race fixture were added. The independent re-read returned **PASS for the revised provisional specification**, with the explicit limit that a local lock cannot prevent out-of-band Dashboard actions; those require processor comparison and escalation.

This result does not establish tax reversal, exact total returned to the customer, applicability to disputes/nonstandard charges, qualified consumer-term wording, configured live Price/payment mapping or a working refund workflow. D06 final review and D07/D11/D12 remain open; CFP-1 stays active (`specified`), CFP-2/3 blocked and G8 active. No runtime, Stripe, payment, account or production setting changed.
