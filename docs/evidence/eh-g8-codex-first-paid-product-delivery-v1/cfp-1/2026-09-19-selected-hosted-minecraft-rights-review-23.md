## Reviewer inputs and requested disposition

Review the current [Minecraft EULA](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/eula) “Using mods” terms and [Usage Guidelines](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/usage-guidelines) “Servers and hosting” and “Extended functionality and modifications” terms against the exact offer. The guidelines allow some server monetization subject to conditions, but the selected product uses a separately installed local companion plus a hosted coordination service; server rules alone do not classify that arrangement. The guidelines also address indirect outside-product access checks affecting in-game features. This packet makes no legal inference that the selected offer is permitted or prohibited.

The reviewer needs the selected O-02 operations, final claims and customer flow; exact EXE, Fabric player/sensor/core JARs, separately provisioned game profile and any Paper/server variant; supported client and personal transport; room/entitlement/API/connector action graph for trial, paid, expired and handoff states; and first-party ownership, notices and prior-publication evidence.
