# CFP-1 suspended-room slot and sponsor accounting reconciliation — 2026-09-20

Program gate: G8 — Environment-harness release evaluation. CFP-1 remains active (`specified`); CFP-2/3 remain blocked. This is a provisional first-cohort **accounting and test rule**, not a final customer limit, current room behavior, entitlement implementation, qualified financial/privacy return or stage promotion.

The owner selected provisional [one-room, one-guest, one-program and ten-verified-effect trial targets](2026-09-19-provisional-hosted-trial-limits-owner-selection-48.md), while the [finite room-activity assay direction](2026-09-20-cfp1-finite-room-activity-assay-direction-216.md) introduces an idle/suspended state. Without a slot rule, an implementation could treat a suspended open room as absent, admit another room, then resume both; without sponsor-term accounting, close/recreate could also reset the seven-day trial's effects or internal PBT. Neither behavior follows the selected host-level trial boundary.

The [trial lifecycle](../../../work-packets/eh-g8-cfp1-hosted-trial-lifecycle-decision-v1.md) now proposes that an open suspended room occupies the sponsor's one concurrent room slot. Explicit close frees only that slot. The verified sponsor's trial clock, successful-effect count, pending uncertain effects and cumulative PBT survive close, browser/device changes and room recreation. Room return checks current identity, sponsor capacity, membership and program visibility; an action grant is checked separately before a new effect. At an explicit handoff, the successor's own room capacity is checked atomically and the predecessor's earlier cost/effect liabilities stay with the origin term. Owner stop/revoke and native release do not depend on an active room lease.

The [guest ingress](../../../work-packets/eh-g8-cfp1-hosted-guest-request-ingress-v1.md), [participant lifecycle](../../../work-packets/eh-g8-cfp1-hosted-participant-entitlement-lifecycle-v1.md), [handoff](../../../work-packets/eh-g8-cfp1-host-handoff-protocol-v1.md), [D07 assay](../../../work-packets/eh-g8-cfp1-d07-provider-unit-allocation-and-assay-v1.md) and [D11 F-07 question](../../../work-packets/eh-g8-cfp1-privacy-and-financial-review-submission-v1.md) use this proposed rule. The dependent [CFP-3.COMMERCE](../../../work-packets/eh-g8-cfp3-software-commerce-v1.md) and [CFP-3.LICENSE](../../../work-packets/eh-g8-cfp3-license-enforcement-v1.md) packets now require suspended-return, second-client, close/recreate and successor-at-capacity fixtures before a commercial room limit may be accepted.
