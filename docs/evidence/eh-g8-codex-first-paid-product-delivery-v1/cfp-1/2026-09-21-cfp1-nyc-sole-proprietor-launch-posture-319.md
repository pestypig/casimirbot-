## CFP launch rule

The seller structure is therefore **selected as a sole proprietorship for the
first bounded route**, with these conditions:

1. CFP-1 and CFP-2 may continue specification and private technical work without
   an LLC.
2. Before CFP-5 activates an attended paid pilot, D11 must return the exact
   legal/assumed-name, sales-tax, income-recordkeeping, merchant/seller,
   customer-term and liability/insurance conditions. Required registrations
   must be evidenced, and Stripe/domain/receipt identity must agree.
3. General or unattended paid availability is not inherited from a bounded
   pilot. Reopen the entity and insurance decision before that transition, and
   also when adding employees or contractors, admitting new jurisdictions,
   materially broader program effects, enterprise contracts, outside capital,
   or a material incident or claim.
4. An EIN may be obtained when required or useful for banking/state/processor
   administration, but it is not treated as proof of a separate legal entity or
   as a substitute for the assumed-name, tax or liability review.

No account, tax, payment, legal-entity, insurance, runtime or production setting
was changed. D07, D11 and D12 remain open; CFP-1 remains active at `specified`.
