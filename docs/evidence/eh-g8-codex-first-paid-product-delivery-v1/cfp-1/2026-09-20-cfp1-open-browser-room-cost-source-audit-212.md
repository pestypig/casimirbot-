`client/src/components/helix/ask-console/shared-live-room/useSharedLiveRoomSync.ts` sets successful foreground room/frame refreshes to 3/5 seconds, background refreshes to 15/30 seconds, and presence updates to 15 seconds in either mode. The hook remains active while it has an active room; closing the dialog passes `foreground=false` from `HelixAskSharedLiveRoomControls.tsx` but does not stop the background timers. A successful nominal background client therefore issues **240 room reads + 120 frame reads + 240 presence posts = 600 requests per hour**; two continuously open clients would issue 1,200/hour or 201,600 across seven days. Foreground nominal cadence is 2,160 requests/hour/client. These are arithmetic scenarios, not measured traffic: scheduling occurs after responses, and failures, browser throttling, page exit or network loss alter the count. The focused `useSharedLiveRoomSync.spec.tsx` suite passed all five tests, including background cadence and non-overlapping pending heartbeats.

The current `/api/agi/realtime/rooms/:roomId` route reads the room through `readSharedRealtimeRoom`, whose `room-store/rooms.ts` queries the database. The visual-frame route calls `readMembership` before returning in-memory frames, and `room-store/rooms.ts` queries membership. The presence route calls `updateSharedRealtimeRoomPresence`, whose `room-store/participants.ts` updates the membership row and refreshes room readiness inside a transaction even when presence remains `present`. The current client therefore causes database work at intervals shorter than five minutes during a continuously successful open-room episode. [Replit's current billing guide](https://docs.replit.com/billing/about-usage-based-billing) says production database compute remains active while requests arrive and for five minutes after the last request.

It is not a per-host marginal charge or a future price: database activity overlaps across members and other workloads, the provider class/rate can change, and the selected [first-party no-model guest page](../../../work-packets/eh-g8-cfp1-hosted-guest-request-ingress-v1.md) does not yet exist. The current experimental room also has model/voice coupling, so its cadence is a **stress comparator**, not a proxy proving the new room's cost.

**D07 consequence:** a one- or two-active-room-hour sample does not cover a browser left open across a seven-day trial. The selected room workload must test a foreground active pair, dialog-closed/background pair, unattended open page, suspended room and return. Freeze a finite idle/lease policy and quantify whether and when database-backed refresh/presence stop, or conservatively cost sustained open-room behavior with an enforceable PBT and per-resource bound. Idle suspension must not reset the seven-day calendar, successful-effect count, owner grant or sponsor budget; resuming must recheck current identity, room/term capacity and action authority. This audit selects no idle threshold, price or PBT allowance.
