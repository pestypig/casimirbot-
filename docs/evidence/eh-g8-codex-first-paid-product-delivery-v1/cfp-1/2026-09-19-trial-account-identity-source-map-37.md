## Contract consequence to freeze in CFP-1

The owner-selected seven-day trial has **no card requirement and no automatic charge**. The host sponsors invited authenticated guests only for trial operations separately selected in O-05. A logical trial record must carry the verified internal sponsor account, trial/benefit version, authoritative start/end, eligibility revision, one-time/reuse state and sanitized audit reference. These are proposed logical fields, not approval to add another table. A start request must have a selected explicit trigger, be idempotent under retry, and never restart the seven-day clock because of a second browser session, provider login, device, room or callback. A replay, old database snapshot or account switch cannot reinstate eligibility. An active trial still supplies only hosted-service eligibility, not membership, consent, a model account or program-owner action grant.

O-05 must choose the exact trial benefit set, trigger, reuse/anti-abuse scope, outage clock rule, in-flight expiry and explicit paid conversion. O-07 must choose the account-deletion and minimum necessary retention/recovery treatment for trial-consumed evidence. Those choices interact: deleting and re-creating an account must have a specified outcome without relying on email matching or silently keeping an undeclared identity marker. The account/privacy owner and qualified reviewer should assess the selected retention and identity link; this source map makes no legal claim about what may be retained.

CFP-3.COMMERCE/LICENSE need negative fixtures for concurrent trial-start requests (one start/end/consumption record), two Google sign-ins with the same verified subject, linked alternate provider, same email/different subjects, canceled sign-in, deleted-then-returned account under both original and formerly linked providers, lost/reinstalled node, duplicate trial-start request, expired trial, database outage/restore, stale entitlement revision, forged checkout success and trial-to-paid conversion without authorization. Starting trial must post no credit, invoke no payment checkout and collect no card. The existing DOM-02/03/05/06 and ENT-01/03/05/08 cases own these checks; no new acceptance scale is created. Free personal access and authorized stop/revoke remain independent of trial status.

**Stage decision:** CFP-1 remains **active (specification)**; CFP-2/3 remain blocked. This inspection identifies a concrete account/deletion dependency but selects no trial benefit, reuse rule or retention period and accepts no production behavior.
