## Derived workload bound and required test

The [D07 assay](../../../work-packets/eh-g8-cfp1-d07-provider-unit-allocation-and-assay-v1.md) now records aggregate admitted active minutes `H`, disjoint admitted intervals `K`, lease causes and room-origin database request endpoints for one seven-day sponsor trial. **Only if** every room-origin database request stops within its finite admitted interval and suspension causes no hidden room write, the room-caused production-database active-time union has a conservative ceiling of `min(H + 5K, 10,085)` minutes. This follows by enclosing each interval plus its five-minute provider idle tail and taking the union; the 10,085-minute ceiling includes the final five-minute tail after the 10,080-minute trial. Overlap can only lower union duration. Actual incremental expense still requires a matched background B run, provider rate and all other meter categories.

With one nonrenewed five-minute or fifteen-minute lease per separated visit, the **database-time-only** ceilings are 100 or 200 minutes for ten visits and 300 or 600 minutes for thirty visits. The ten-visit case carries ten verified effects; the thirty-visit case also carries only ten verified effects. These durations are engineering probes, not customer allowances or PBT totals. An intentionally sustained room remains an adverse case. The test must show that room-origin work stops after suspension and an intentional return reserves cost capacity before dispatch. If not, the formula is inapplicable and D07 must carry a sourced sustained-open bound.

The essential product implication is that a ten-effect trial quota alone cannot bound hosting expense: failed actions, requests that yield no effect and repeated intentional returns still consume resources. The [pilot assay](../../../work-packets/eh-g8-cfp1-hosted-pilot-price-and-capacity-assay-v1.md) and PBT contract now require cumulative sponsor-term PBT reservations, separate hard request/compute/egress/storage limits, and a deployment-wide cohort guard. Suspended open rooms retain the provisional concurrent room slot but do not automatically renew a cost-incurring lease. Cost-based tokens are an internal admission mechanism; the final price, token allowance, idle threshold and user-facing wording remain D07 decisions after B/P/T measurement or sourced bounds and qualified financial/privacy review.
