## Recommendation and reason

For the **first hosted review and B/P/T cost cohort**, use a U.S.-located sponsoring purchaser/host and a U.S.-located invited guest, with the same target for the no-card action-inclusive trial. This narrows the first pilot's reviewer and processor scope while preserving the separate free personal MCP entitlement. The owner's earlier billing-location reply suggested U.S. customers without selecting a market; the later seller reply locates the individual seller in New York City. Neither reply establishes a global paid entitlement or a final U.S.-only rule.

[Stripe's U.S. tax guide](https://docs.stripe.com/tax/supported-countries/united-states) says applicable tax rules and rates vary by region, and its [customer-location guide](https://docs.stripe.com/tax/customer-locations) requires a recognized location for automatic calculation. [New York's computer-software bulletin](https://www.tax.ny.gov/pubs_and_bulls/tg_bulletins/st/computer_software.htm) makes the purchaser's use location relevant if the offer includes remotely accessed prewritten software; the mixed CasimirBot offer remains unclassified. The [European Commission's place-of-taxation guide](https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/taxation/vat/vat-directive/place-taxation_en) illustrates that a wider electronic-services market needs a different jurisdiction analysis. These sources support scoping the review, not a self-issued tax or sales-rights opinion.

The first U.S. cohort makes the existing domestic-card $5/$10 calculations relevant **illustrations**, but a U.S. address does not guarantee a domestic-issued card or a particular tax rate. Include international-card/changed-location sensitivity in D07. The no-card trial requires a separately reviewed location-evidence path without a payment method. D11 must decide what counts as host/guest location, whether travel or cross-border guest actions are allowed, how to handle conflicting evidence, which data are retained and which tax/rights/privacy registrations or notices are required. The paid/trial shared-action fixture should include a U.S. host and guest; out-of-scope buyer/guest and changed-location cases should be denial or review fixtures rather than silent admission.
