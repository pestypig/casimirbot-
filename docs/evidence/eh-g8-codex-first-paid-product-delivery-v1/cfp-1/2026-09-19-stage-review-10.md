## Evidence-based stage decision

**Keep CFP-1 active as specification work. Do not dispatch CFP-2/3 or claim
commercial or release acceptance.** This turn materially advanced the hosted
participant policy and refreshed the rights/distribution question for the
selected offer, but owner commercial terms and independent rights disposition
remain missing. The one-player NAV-EQ game qualification is a separately
admitted parallel G8 lane; it cannot be used as evidence that CFP-1 or CFP-2
closed.

The next reviewable owner decision is the payer/guest scope: host sponsorship
with authenticated guests is recommended because payment can stay separate
from each guest's identity and program grant, but no option is treated as
selected without an explicit owner answer. The product owner must also choose
the initial hosted benefit/price mapping and lifecycle terms using measured
cost inputs. The rights reviewer needs the exact revised payment-to-Minecraft
interaction map and first-party contribution evidence. The release owner needs
a coherent selected artifact and notice/feed disposition. Until those outcomes
are recorded, this stage decision remains open rather than complete or blocked.
