# CFP-1 hosted payer and guest selection — 2026-09-19

Status: dated product-owner decision capture after the [September 14 owner register](2026-09-14-platform-account-08/owner-decisions.json) and the [seven-day trial selection](2026-09-19-hosted-trial-owner-selection-20.md). No account, room, payment, repository or release setting changed.

In response to “Who should own the seven-day hosted trial and later subscription for a shared room? Each guest would still need their own account, consent and program grants; payment would never share a Codex subscription,” the owner selected **“One host sponsors invited guests (recommended).”** This resolves the O-01 payer/guest *model*: the authenticated room host owns the relevant trial or subscription, and an authenticated invited guest does not need a separate purchase merely to join within the eventual room limits. A guest's personal MCP tools remain independently free.

The room's hosted-service eligibility is derived from the current verified sponsor state and the selected operation, not from a client-supplied `host` flag, invitation alone, account type or a guest's subscription. The invitation and membership still decide whether that guest may enter; their processing consent and each program owner's exact grant still decide what may be seen or done. Neither sponsor payment nor a trial shares a Codex/GPT account or grants a player action. A guest may be a program owner without becoming the payer, and a payer may be unable to act on a guest's program.

The initial host-transfer policy, seat/room/node limits, trial start/reuse rule, benefit set, active-operation response to sponsor expiry/loss, and retention/recovery clocks remain unselected. No automatic sponsor switch is implied. The room/account owners must freeze a race-safe transfer or closure protocol and test host cancellation, refund, lost session, account switch and guest disconnect before CFP-3/4 implementation acceptance. The R-MC-01 reviewer must evaluate the sponsor-to-guest-to-Minecraft effect path for both active trial and paid term, as well as the independent free personal path. This decision does not grant commercial permission or admit a downstream stage.
