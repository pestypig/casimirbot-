## Terms and distribution questions, not conclusions

The current official [Minecraft EULA](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/eula) treats original Java tools/plugins as Mods, permits distribution of a Mod rather than a combined Modded Version, and restricts selling or trying to make money from original Java Mods. The current [Usage Guidelines](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/usage-guidelines) include an extended-modification condition concerning direct **or indirect** checks for access to outside products or services that affect in-game functions. Their server-hosting section separately allows certain server charges under its conditions. **Inference for review:** permission to charge for a Minecraft server does not by itself answer whether this local Fabric connector plus separately paid coordination/action service fits those conditions. The packet does not decide permitted versus prohibited conduct; obtain a qualified, dated interpretation of the selected implementation and, where the applicable terms do not clearly permit it, written permission or a documented authorized arrangement before including the dependent paid claim.

The proposed package graph is CasimirBot signed EXE/service and separately provisioned Fabric player/sensor/connector-core JARs, a user-owned Minecraft game/account, and selected Fabric/Java components. The [component rights matrix](rights-draft-2026-09-06/rights-component-matrix.md) found local JARs but not those JARs in the inspected standard EXE resource tree; this is **not** a final shipping manifest. The owner plans to remove navigation evaluation mods before release; final EXE, JAR, launcher/profile, updater and separately downloaded bytes must prove that exclusion. Connector origin and distribution notices, first-party [owner statement](2026-09-19-first-party-authorship-owner-attestation-29.md), prior MIT publication and the actual license terms of each shipped dependency require their separate R-OWN/R-BIN/NOTICE/LGPL/ASSET/PUBLIC/MAP review. Do not bundle or redistribute game software or imply Mojang/Microsoft endorsement from this plan.
