# Minecraft official-terms review branches — 2026-09-19

Program gate: G8 — Environment-harness release evaluation. CFP-1 remains **active (specification)** and CFP-2/3 **blocked**. Read-only review of the live [Minecraft EULA](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/eula) and [Minecraft Usage Guidelines](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/usage-guidelines) on 2026-09-19. The pages did not supply a stable edition identifier in the inspected text; a qualified reviewer must capture the applicable terms again for the final design and release date. This is a question map, not legal advice, written permission or a commercial disposition.

| Official section and observed rule | Exact CFP-1 question for qualified review |
| --- | --- |
| EULA, “Using Mods”: an original Java modification may be distributed apart from a combined modified game, but the EULA restricts selling or trying to make money from original Mods. | Does the separately provisioned Fabric connector plus paid/trial hosted guest-action relationship count as making money from a Mod, even when personal use and the JAR download are free? What package, source and claim changes or written permission would resolve it? |
| Guidelines, “Extended functionality and modifications”: a Mod may not directly or indirectly verify access to an outside product or service in a way that affects in-game functions. | Classify the entire hosted-eligibility → guest delegation → broker → Fabric effect path. Does checking a sponsor's CasimirBot subscription or trial before admitting a guest's in-game action fall within this condition, despite no billing check inside the JAR? |
| Guidelines, “Servers and hosting”: charging for access to a Minecraft server is conditional. The section defines a server by one connecting address/IP, requires a genuine game copy, limits outside-product control of server access, and attaches same-price, enabled-Mod access and server-control conditions to charging for entry. | Is the proposed host-owned local Fabric player plus separately hosted CasimirBot room actually a charged Minecraft server under this section? If someone argues that it is, document each applicable condition against the real network/control topology; do not infer approval from unrelated paid servers. Separately evaluate any gameplay-affecting entitlement clause without assuming it overrides the Mod conditions. |
| Guidelines, essential/naming and “Constructed promotions”: branding must not imply Mojang/Microsoft endorsement; the policy places conditions on name, disclaimer and use of gameplay to promote products unrelated to Minecraft. The video/stream/screenshot section adds its own conditions. | Review the actual landing page, installer, screenshots, demo video, feature heading and compatibility wording. Is CasimirBot's broader multi-program subscription “unrelated” for the constructed-promotion rule when Minecraft gameplay is used to sell it? Does a technical demo, free personal page or paid CTA need different wording, a disclaimer, a different asset, or written approval? |

These branches are independent. Permission to distribute an original Mod does not automatically permit charging for the connected service; a server-charge allowance does not automatically classify this architecture as a server; and permission for an action does not automatically clear its advertising materials. The [R-MC-01 submission](../../../work-packets/eh-g8-cfp1-minecraft-commercial-rights-review-submission-v1.md) should ask for a context-by-context disposition and evidence/permission scope covering free personal, no-card trial, paid guest effect, expiry/refund, handoff and each proposed public claim. The product owner then chooses an admitted design or the non-game fallback. No release or marketing claim is approved by this checkpoint.
