# CFP-1 Auth0 passkey versus MFA source screen — 2026-09-20

Program gate: **G8 — Environment-harness release evaluation**. CFP-1 remains active (`specified`); CFP-2/3 remain blocked. This official-source screen follows the [authenticated current-plan read](2026-09-20-cfp1-auth0-current-plan-and-mfa-entitlement-read-277.md). It assesses whether the displayed Free-plan passkey entitlement is a ready replacement for the proposed Auth0 Pro MFA challenge; it does not configure or test a factor.

| Official source | Supported conclusion | First-customer consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Auth0 Universal Login experience](https://auth0.com/docs/authenticate/login/auth0-universal-login/universal-login-vs-classic-login/universal-experience) and [database connections](https://auth0.com/docs/authenticate/database-connections) | Auth0 describes passkeys as an authentication method for **database connections** and Google as a separate social connection. | The Free plan's passkey row does not establish a WebAuthn factor for the existing Google social identity or the current CasimirBot Google-only profile. |
| [Auth0 passwordless authentication on database connections](https://auth0.com/docs/authenticate/database-connections/passwordless-authentication-for-db-connect) | A database-connection passkey needs an alternative configured login method such as email or phone; passkey-only authentication is not supported. | A proposed linked database identity would add enrollment, alternate-method and recovery obligations. Email equality alone cannot authorize linking it to an existing Google-owned CasimirBot profile. |
| [Auth0 step-up authentication](https://auth0.com/docs/secure/multi-factor-authentication/step-up-authentication) and [WebAuthn security-key MFA](https://auth0.com/docs/secure/multi-factor-authentication/fido-authentication-with-webauthn/configure-webauthn-security-keys-for-mfa) | Auth0 documents MFA claims/challenges for sensitive access; WebAuthn **as MFA** has plan and login-implementation availability conditions. The docs do not prove a factor event after CasimirBot's specific operation intent. | A passkey used as primary database login is not automatically an operation-time, second-factor event bound to a CasimirBot enrollment or payment intent. |

**Decision for qualification order:** keep the existing linked Auth0 MFA seam as the first **technical** candidate because source and deterministic tests already exist. Given the observed Free-plan Pro MFA exclusion and over-limit warning, its CFP-1 cost case must include the displayed paid-plan branch, without purchasing or selecting that plan yet. Treat a linked Auth0 database passkey as a distinct alternative, not a free drop-in replacement: prove trusted initial link from the Google-owned profile, new challenge after the sensitive intent, stable subject, recovery after passkey loss, provider availability and the cost of its required alternate method. A first-party WebAuthn authenticator is another distinct alternative with its own trusted enrollment/recovery and maintenance cost. Neither alternative may be advertised or assigned zero total cost merely because a plan table says passkeys are included.

[Auth0's current public plan comparison](https://auth0.com/pricing) lists passkeys on Free while excluding Pro MFA Factors from that plan; the [account observation](2026-09-20-cfp1-auth0-current-plan-and-mfa-entitlement-read-277.md) separately records the owner's actual current tier and warning. Feature labels do not resolve the required CasimirBot proof flow or final account charge.

If the selected factor cannot satisfy the ordinary-user Google-only positive and denial cases in [D02](../../../work-packets/eh-g8-cfp1-google-p1-fresh-auth-feasibility-v1.md), retain the P1 onboarding claim hold and revise the identity policy explicitly; do not weaken the operation-bound proof by treating a Google account chooser, recently issued token or already-open Auth0 session as a fresh factor event. D07 must price the route that actually passes. This screen supplies no accepted step-up, account/security sign-off, D07 SKU or provider bill, and changes no tenant, security, billing, runtime or production setting.
