# Subscription-only offer reconciliation

Purpose: apply owner decision D15 to current CFP-1 and candidate implementation handoffs. This is a specification supplement, not commercial clearance, frozen terms, implementation evidence or a stage ledger. The canonical work program retains stage authority.

## Controlling scope

- The initial purchase is a recurring subscription for selected CasimirBot harness capabilities, maintained integrations, updates and support.
- The supported external reasoning application supplies model access under the user's own account. No credit purchase, bundled inference allowance, managed API enrollment or CasimirBot-funded GPT Live belongs to the initial offer.
- Applicable Stripe subscription/account/webhook foundations may be reused. Existing prepaid fixtures describe earlier implementation; they do not dictate current checkout products.
- Historical decision D14 records the source fixtures, not a requirement to map the current $5 option to credits. D15 controls initial-offer scope and supersedes D10's optional provider-service wording for launch.
- Minecraft remains the visible technical proof of concept for the broader harness. This correction does not clear the proposed commercial connector relationship or reduce the broader G8 scope.

## Entitlement acceptance reconciliation

The earlier entitlement/distribution draft remains an evidence snapshot. The following meanings control its ENT scenario IDs for the initial subscription offer. These are candidate tests, not executed or frozen acceptance results.

| ID | Initial subscription scenario | Required outcome |

All identity, safety, consent, privacy, revocation and software lifecycle requirements remain. No credit balance or provider-spend lease is a launch prerequisite. Tests of pre-existing provider ledger behavior are regression checks only where implementation touches shared code; their existence does not re-admit provider products.

Managed inference is excluded by product scope, not assumed free or profitable. Actual hosting/support costs and the benefits of the reported $5/$10 presets remain unverified. Earlier numerical proposals remain proposals. Agents must not ask the owner to choose credits again or implement historical credit products as default subscription benefits.
