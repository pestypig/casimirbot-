The reviewer verified the four recorded source hashes and the [official Codex MCP documentation](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/extend/mcp?surface=cli): local HTTP header helper, cached headers, one refresh on same-origin POST 401/403 only when values change, and precedence of explicit bearer/OAuth credentials over helper-provided `Authorization`.

The first review found three required corrections. A helper-time listener check cannot protect a **cached** bearer after loopback port takeover; device/client fields do not prove the sending process or stop a same-user caller from invoking the helper; and a Codex 401/403 retry during a mutating POST needs pre-dispatch rejection or durable request/result deduplication. The living contract was corrected with an explicit transport/process trust decision, takeover-after-caching and same-user replay fixtures, and P1C-09 automatic-retry/native-effect reconciliation.

**Final review verdict: PASS for the revised specification boundaries.** The reviewer confirmed that the packet now treats the HTTP helper as conditional, with no assertion that signing, loopback, audience binding or a short token alone proves listener/process identity. This PASS does **not** establish an authenticated transport or a working ordinary-user grant. Account/security owners must freeze the threat boundary, lifetime and transport design; CFP-2 must pass signed installed positive and negative cases before any P1 customer claim. D02/D12 and CFP-1 exit remain open.
