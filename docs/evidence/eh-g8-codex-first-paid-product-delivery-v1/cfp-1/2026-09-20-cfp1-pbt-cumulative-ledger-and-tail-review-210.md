# CFP-1 cumulative PBT ledger and delayed-cost review — 2026-09-20

Program gate: G8 — Environment-harness release evaluation. CFP-1 remains active (`specified`); CFP-2/3 remain blocked. This is an internal accounting-specification correction, not a measured B/P/T cost case, selected price/allowance, present ledger, financial disposition or customer claim.

The [PBT contract](../../../work-packets/eh-g8-cfp1-hosted-resource-budget-token-contract-v1.md) previously stated a per-term ceiling but left ambiguous how individual reservations and settlements round. A per-item ceiling would turn ten $0.0001 work items into ten PBT even though their combined modeled cost is $0.001. A local Decimal check returned `naive_per_item_pbt=10`, `cumulative_pbt=1`, `cost_usd=0.0010`, `residual_usd=0.0000`. The revised contract retains provider-precision fixed-point cost, rounds the **cumulative sponsor-term** balance, atomically includes all pending upper bounds before dispatch and separately enforces finite per-resource ceilings. It defines a concrete two-instance last-token race, replay versus genuinely new failed work, and settlement/release evidence.

The first independent read-only review found two specification ambiguities: causal provider usage billed after room close or host handoff, and a provider-rate rise during an active term. The revised [D07 assay](../../../work-packets/eh-g8-cfp1-d07-provider-unit-allocation-and-assay-v1.md) and [CFP-3 commerce handoff](../../../work-packets/eh-g8-cfp3-software-commerce-v1.md) now hold the origin sponsor's database idle tail and delayed network/storage liability until reconciliation, conserve overlapping provider units, and keep a closed term reconcilable without restoring action authority. The active-term modeled rate/conversion and allowance remain pinned; actual provider-rate variance is separately recorded as operator exposure and a reviewed model change applies to future terms under the accepted customer terms. The contract contains explicit `t=2` to `t=7` handoff-tail and `r1` to `r2` rate fixtures.

The same independent reviewer rechecked the corrected paragraphs and returned **PASS** for arithmetic, atomic reservation, origin attribution, rate treatment, subscription/no-card boundaries and CFP-3 fixture handoff. This is a specification review, not an audited provider-cost result or qualified financial/privacy return. D07 still needs sourced B/P/T load or per-input conservative bounds, account/fee and cohort sensitivity, a useful numeric trial/paid capacity and owner selection. D11/D12 and CFP-1 remain open.
