# CFP-1 small-cohort signing-cost sensitivity — 2026-09-20

Program gate: **G8 — Environment-harness release evaluation**. CFP-1 remains active (`specified`); CFP-2/3 remain blocked. This is public-rate scenario arithmetic, not a live Stripe fee, a selected signing provider, a D07 margin, a tax opinion or a customer price.

Use the pilot assay's **hypothetical** one monthly domestic-card payment per sponsor: [Stripe Payments standard pricing](https://stripe.com/pricing) of 2.9% + $0.30 and [Stripe Billing pay-as-you-go pricing](https://stripe.com/billing/pricing) of 0.7% of Billing volume. This yields an unrounded scenario of `$9.34` after these modeled fees for a `$10` subscription and `$4.52` for `$5`. [Microsoft's Windows code-signing options](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/apps/package-and-deploy/code-signing-options) quote **approximately $9.99/month** for Azure Artifact Signing. Use that amount only as a dated **one-product fixed-cost example**; the owner's actual provider, account rate, term and tax are not established.

| Paying sponsors in one month | Modeled fee-net receipts at $10 each | After one illustrative $9.99 signing charge | Modeled fee-net receipts at $5 each | After one illustrative $9.99 signing charge |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1 | $9.34 | **−$0.65** | $4.52 | **−$5.47** |
| 2 | $18.68 | $8.69 | $9.04 | **−$0.95** |
| 10 | $93.40 | $83.41 | $45.20 | $35.21 |

Arithmetic: `N × [price − (0.029 × price + 0.30) − (0.007 × price)] − 9.99`. The processor may round actual fees per payment; a real account may have other rates and charges. The last columns are **before** Replit, identity, distribution, support, refunds, disputes, nonrecoverable tax, free-user expense and all paid/trial variable work. Ten sponsors and thirty trial starts would leave at most `$83.41` of the `$10` scenario to cover all those other costs and any desired contribution after the illustrative signing line. For a 20%-of-gross-revenue contribution sensitivity, only `$63.41` remains for those other costs. This is not evidence that ten sponsors suffice.

The product-wide view records the signing expense once. A hosted-only contribution view may allocate part of that shared cost to free personal distribution, but must show the allocation and reconcile back to the whole-product total; it cannot omit the charge or count it twice. The real D07 decision still needs matched B/P/T work or sourced upper bounds for every material cost category, observed or contractually established fees, trial-start and low-paying-host sensitivities, selected capacity, and a qualified financial/privacy return. No runtime, provider, account, publication, Stripe or customer state changed.
