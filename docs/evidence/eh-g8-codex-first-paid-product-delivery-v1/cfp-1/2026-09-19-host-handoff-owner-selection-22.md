# CFP-1 explicit host handoff selection — 2026-09-19

Status: dated capture of a product-owner decision and narrow current-source feasibility finding. It follows the [host-sponsorship selection](2026-09-19-host-sponsorship-owner-selection-21.md); the [CFP-1 contract](../../../work-packets/eh-g8-cfp1-product-rights-and-offer-contract-v1.md) and [G8 work program](../../../helix-environment-harness-work-program-v1.md) retain product and stage authority. No runtime, room, billing or deployment setting changed.

In response to “If a sponsoring host leaves or loses hosted eligibility, how should an existing room continue? A guest’s own account or program grant would never transfer automatically,” the owner selected **“Allow explicit handoff to another eligible host (recommended).”** Thus an eligible invited member may become the room's new sponsor only through a reviewed, explicit transfer. No guest's account, model subscription, processing consent, player authority, program grant or private history transfers with sponsorship. No automatic sponsor reassignment is authorized.

At inspected Desktop HEAD `256554ca2637b2978a83616d9f9670069fdfd8c4`, `server/services/helix-ask/realtime-room/room-store/rooms.ts` creates the owner in slot one. The inspected `leaveOrCloseSharedRealtimeRoom` path in `room-store/participants.ts` closes the room when the owner leaves, revoking active invitations, source bindings/credentials, adapter admissions and subject bindings; non-owner leave only removes that member and their subject bindings. The targeted room-store and shared contract search found no current owner/sponsor transfer operation. This is a source-level finding, not an exhaustive installed-client result. Existing owner leave must not be described as seamless handoff.

The dependent room/account/commerce implementation must define an authenticated, race-safe handoff protocol with current sponsor identity and current eligible successor verification. It must distinguish a billing-sponsor change from room-owner/mission-lead and individual program ownership, and either migrate each role explicitly or close/recreate the room when a role cannot safely move. The transition must invalidate old sponsor-derived hosted grants, re-evaluate every member/source/consent/effect lease, audit both approval and acceptance, reject replay or simultaneous competing transfers, and keep stop/revoke available throughout. If the sponsor becomes ineligible before handoff completes, new hosted operations fail closed; permitted history/recovery and independently authorized personal tools remain available. No affected in-flight effect may outlive its separately frozen release bound.

The room/account owners should review and freeze the following **proposed** cases under existing ENT-02/03/08, COLLAB-04/05 and DOM-03/05 rather than invent a separate release ladder. Successor membership and the exact moment their trial or paid term must be active are protocol decisions, not owner selections in this capture:

| Proposed case | Acceptance boundary to freeze |
| --- | --- |
| Eligible successor | A separately authenticated candidate who satisfies the frozen membership and hosted-eligibility rules explicitly accepts sponsorship; the room records one new sponsor revision. The predecessor's remaining paid/trial time is not copied or added to the successor's term. |
| Ineligible or wrong successor | Absent membership, expired trial, canceled/refunded term, wrong account or wrong room cannot receive sponsorship even if the former host requests it. No room or program authority changes. |
| Race and replay | Two simultaneous successors, duplicate accept, stale approval, reconnect and restart yield at most one accepted sponsor revision. Old host-derived hosted admission fails after the revision changes. |
| Host loss before completion | Cancel/refund/leave/account loss before successor acceptance blocks new hosted work; allowed history/stop/revoke remain accessible. The reviewed recovery path decides who can finish or restart handoff; no silent candidate promotion. |
| Program ownership and effects | Each contributor's program grant and processing consent remain separately attributable. Effect admission rechecks the current sponsor, member, source, subject and native lease; uncertain in-flight work cannot replay after transfer. |
| Current leave behavior | Owner leave still closes and revokes under current source. The new path must prove an explicit handoff is accepted **before** the old owner leave is treated as continuity, or report closure truthfully. |

The owner has selected *that* explicit handoff is supported, not its exact initiator/acceptor, lost-host recovery authority, transfer deadline or data-retention policy. Those values require a frozen protocol and negative fixtures before CFP-3/4 dispatch. The first advertised benefit set and room limits are still unselected. R-MC-01 must include trial/paid sponsor handoff in the payment-to-Minecraft-effect classification. This decision does not clear rights or admit an implementation stage.
