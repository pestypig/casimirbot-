The migration list above is the first-offer account/room/connector/billing path, **not** every profile-dependent subsystem. The full deletion inventory at CFP-3 dispatch must also classify dependent records and external credentials from the active migration graph, including later voice/party and any provider or connector integrations actually shipped. An `ON DELETE CASCADE` clause describes hard deletion of a parent row; it is not evidence of soft-delete cleanup, provider cancellation or native stop.

Required negative acceptance: delete a room owner with an active invitation, source credential, connected device and in-flight bounded action; delete an invited guest with a grant; retry deletion after a partial/uncertain result; sign in again through the original and previously linked verified providers; restore a stale database snapshot; and try an old ingress token, room membership, device trust, paid term and action lease. Observe which work actually stopped and which remains uncertain. A fresh account may regain only separately authenticated and reauthorized personal access; no historical room, paid, consent or program-effect authority follows from recognizing its identity. Keep authenticated safety stop/revoke and reviewed export/recovery available under their own rules. Test processor cancellation/retention only in an authorized sandbox; no live payment action is implied.

**Stage effect:** CFP-1 remains active (specification), CFP-2/3 blocked. This source map adds a specific deletion/return prerequisite to the selected trial and subscription, but selects no deletion retention, cancellation, handoff deadline or release claim.
