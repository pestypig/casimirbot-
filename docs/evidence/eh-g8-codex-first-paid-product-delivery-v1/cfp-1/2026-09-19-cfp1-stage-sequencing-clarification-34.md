# CFP-1 rights and artifact stage-sequencing clarification — 2026-09-19

Status: dated clarification of the [CFP-1 working contract](../../../work-packets/eh-g8-cfp1-product-rights-and-offer-contract-v1.md), not a rights opinion, release acceptance or stage promotion. The [environment work program](../../../helix-environment-harness-work-program-v1.md) alone controls G8 stage and maturity. The [closure audit](2026-09-19-cfp1-current-closure-audit-18.md) and [shared-action checkpoint](2026-09-19-cfp1-shared-action-stage-addendum-27.md) remain immutable snapshots. Inspected source HEAD: `256554ca2637b2978a83616d9f9670069fdfd8c4`, with a shared dirty worktree. No runtime, signing, installation, payment, publication or repository-visibility action occurred.

The later checkpoint's rights row places a **final EXE/JAR/profile cohort, notices and accepted customer download/update route** under “before CFP-1 closure.” Read literally, that creates a dependency loop: the G8 ledger assigns the signed artifact and reviewed customer route to CFP-3, which cannot start until CFP-1 and CFP-2 close. The current local [artifact coherency check](2026-09-19-local-distribution-artifact-coherency-33.md) also found only mutable, mismatched or unsigned output paths. They cannot substitute for a final cohort.

The [candidate Minecraft rights map](2026-09-19-action-inclusive-minecraft-commercial-rights-map-32.md#exact-reviewer-return-needed-before-r-mc-01-closure) likewise requests signed EXE/JAR/profile hashes before R-MC-01 closure. For CFP-1, the qualified R-MC-01 return must instead identify the exact **planned** connector, action, package/provisioning graph, payment-to-effect terms, permission basis or required written permission, and conditions for later byte-level verification. Signed hashes and final notice conformance are CFP-3 outputs. The dated map remains a useful question set but its final-hash timing does not override the canonical stage order.

The working [MCP offer-context triage](../../../work-packets/eh-g8-cfp1-mcp-offer-context-triage-v1.md) now distinguishes a declared client and frozen catalog tests at CFP-1 from actual installed `tools/list` and effects at CFP-2 and signed-artifact repetition at CFP-3/4.

| Stage | Evidence required at that stage | Stop condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CFP-1 specification closure | Owner-selected personal/hosted operations, connector and contexts; commercial terms; planned source/component/asset/connector/profile and delivery inclusion matrix with selected versions or bounded inclusion rules; qualified R-OWN/R-MC/R-PUBLIC and planned R-BIN/NOTICE/LGPL/ASSET/MAP dispositions; applicable notice/source-offer and evaluation-mod exclusion requirements; frozen release-time tests and independent child-packet review. | Missing commercial permission basis for the selected paid relationship, unknown planned component rights, unresolved owner terms or unfrozen acceptance keeps CFP-1 active. A component with uncertain rights must be excluded from selected shipping scope or remain a hold. |
| CFP-2 useful installed capability | Implement and test the free personal path against CFP-1's accepted scope and declared per-run artifact, including applicable existing PNA/environment prerequisites. | A specification alone does not prove an ordinary-user installed effect. |
| CFP-3 paid entitlement and distributable build | Build one reserved, immutable source/build/EXE/JAR/profile/update cohort; inspect actual dependency and asset closure, SPDX/NOTICE/source-offer obligations and evaluation-mod exclusion; hash and sign exact bytes; verify customer download/update route and entitlement scenarios. | Any unexpected included component, unmet notice, source/byte mismatch, invalid signature or unavailable customer route fails CFP-3 and returns changed scope to rights review. |
| CFP-4 integrated audit | Repeat the frozen matrix on the same signed artifact and accepted web deployment. | No substitution of a prior unpacked, mutable or unsigned build. |

This is a sequencing correction, not a lower rights standard. A qualified reviewer must approve the **selected planned commercial relationship** before CFP-1 closure; a generic “free mod, paid service” label, owner authorship assertion or removal of evaluation mods is insufficient. The exact build may expose obligations not visible in the plan; CFP-3 must reject or return those deltas before release. Actual signed output hashes cannot be prerequisites for the stage that specifies how to create them.

**Stage decision:** CFP-1 remains **active (specification)**. CFP-2 and CFP-3 remain blocked by the canonical ledger. The exact paid action/connector, remaining O-03–07 terms, qualified Minecraft and component rights review, selected planned inclusion matrix and final independent CFP-1 review are still open. This clarification closes only the circular wording; it accepts no shipped artifact or commercial use.
