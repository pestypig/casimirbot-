external-client-first setup; bounded selected-player work; independent software
and model payments; a rights-reviewed source boundary; customer downloads that
do not require source access; explicit recovery/expiry behavior. The exact
commercial relationship is still a proposal because required owner and rights
evidence is absent.

The earlier six-resource task was an agent-generated candidate, not a user
requirement. Source inspection found automatic movement and inventory-baseline
behavior that contradicted its proposed guarantees. The two-target alternative
preserves the user-selected bounded-assistance direction for review; it does
not silently redefine CFP-1 completion or prove the product worth purchasing.

Existing authorized environment work continues under its own canonical packets.
CFP2/3 candidates do not authorize implementation, game changes, payments,
license/source visibility changes, service restarts, signing or publication.
No requests were sent to outside rights holders.
