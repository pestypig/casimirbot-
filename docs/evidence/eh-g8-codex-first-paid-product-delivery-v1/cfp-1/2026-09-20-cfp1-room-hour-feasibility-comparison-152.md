It is a sensitivity, not a B/P/T marginal measurement, all-resource cost bound, account-specific Stripe fee confirmation or selected customer term.

For the existing example cohort of ten paying hosts and 30 no-card trial starts, compare the current **20 paid / two trial active room-hour** assay with a **ten paid / one trial active room-hour** cost-containment measurement point. Both assume the observed single `4 vCPU / 8 GiB` Autoscale instance works at full allocation during nonoverlapping room intervals, each interval adds production-database active time, a 30-day deployment base is charged and each sponsor term adds one five-minute database idle tail. [Replit's publishing guide](https://docs.replit.com/billing/deployment-pricing) describes request-work compute metering, and its [database guide](https://docs.replit.com/billing/about-usage-based-billing) describes the active-time tail. These deliberately adverse assumptions do not represent observed room utilization; they do not bound work outside room intervals, requests, outbound transfer, storage or support.

At the hypothetical $10 monthly amount and the worksheet's public [Stripe domestic-card and Billing fee comparator](../../../work-packets/eh-g8-cfp1-hosted-unit-economics-worksheet-v1.md), the 20/2-hour point fails the illustrative 20%-of-gross contribution sensitivity on this partial resource screen alone. The 10/1-hour point leaves positive **partial** headroom before the unmeasured categories, but its usefulness for ten verified guest effects over a seven-day trial is unproven. The hypothetical $5 monthly comparator fails this partial 20%-contribution screen even at 10/1 hours. A hypothetical higher monthly amount could improve headroom without proving demand, account Price availability, tax/fee terms or all-in viability; no higher amount is selected here.

Retain 20/2 for comparison and add 10/1 to the next B/P/T usefulness and cost measurement. Do not replace the owner's provisional one-room, one-guest, one-program and ten-successful-effect trial targets by this calculation.
