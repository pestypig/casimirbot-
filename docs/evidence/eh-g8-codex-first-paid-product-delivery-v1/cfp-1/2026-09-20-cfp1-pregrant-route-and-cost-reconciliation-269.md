## Reconciled first-cohort proposal

The [first-party API packet](../../../work-packets/eh-g8-cfp1-first-party-guest-action-api-contract-v1.md) now proposes that an authenticated program owner explicitly create one exact, requester-bound, short-lived offer and server-held one-use grant. The guest can view that offer and request it later. The guest request rechecks sponsor, room, member, program, target, connector, grant and resource authority, then atomically consumes the grant with at most one broker admission. An offer or grant alone causes no native effect. Owner revoke, stop and status access remain distinct. The first-party and optional qualified MCP ingress must race against the same one-use grant and native-effect ledger. The [guest ingress](../../../work-packets/eh-g8-cfp1-hosted-guest-request-ingress-v1.md), [claim candidate](../../../work-packets/eh-g8-cfp1-landing-and-account-copy-candidate-v1.md), [decision queue](../../../work-packets/eh-g8-cfp1-owner-decision-and-review-queue-v1.md), [finite-lease probe](../../../work-packets/eh-g8-cfp1-finite-room-lease-candidate-v1.md) and [CFP-3 handoff](../../../work-packets/eh-g8-cfp3-shared-program-action-delegation-v1.md) now use this order. A post-request owner-approval variant is outside this first-cohort cost trace and would require separate UX, authorization, claim and cost review if selected later.

The earlier [API review 266](2026-09-20-cfp1-first-party-guest-action-api-review-266.md), [claim review 267](2026-09-20-cfp1-guest-action-claim-sequence-review-267.md) and [D07 route review 268](2026-09-20-cfp1-d07-first-party-route-workload-review-268.md) passed their then-current **post-request owner-decision** sequence. ## D07 arithmetic and boundary

The [versioned workload fixture](../../../work-packets/eh-g8-cfp1-d07-hosted-budget-workload-fixture-v1.md) now counts owner offer/grant creation, guest request and final result read once per scripted successful effect, plus one guest offer listing per intentional visit. Thus its successful-path browser route components are `3 × effects + visits`: T10/P10 `3 × 10 + 10 = 40`, P50 `3 × 50 + 25 = 175`, P100 `3 × 100 + 25 = 325`. Owner revoke, repeated listing, retries, denials, no-effect, pending status, invitation/join, authentication, target observation, lease, connector, background and provider tails add work. These numbers are neither total requests nor measured cost nor customer limits. The [D07 provider assay](../../../work-packets/eh-g8-cfp1-d07-provider-unit-allocation-and-assay-v1.md) uses the corrected trace but still needs matched B/P/T units or conservative all-input bounds, final limits and financial review.
