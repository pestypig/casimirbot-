# Owner selection: provisional hosted-trial limits — 2026-09-19

The owner answered the seven-day no-card trial-limit question: **“Use these provisional limits (Recommended).”** The presented proposal was **ten successful bounded effects per sponsoring host, one active room, one invited guest, and one connected program**, with the same per-action consent and safety checks. These are provisional product targets for CFP-1 cost and installed validation, not limits currently enforced by the code or final marketing terms. The room's existing two-person maximum is consistent with host plus one guest, but is not proof of the trial entitlement.

For acceptance, count a successful effect only after a native, attributable postcondition is verified; an admission denial, failed attempt, duplicate/retried request, or unverified receipt must not permanently spend the ten-effect allowance. Reserve and settle uncertain in-flight effects atomically against the verified sponsor identity and trial revision so parallel guests/retries cannot exceed the cap or gain an extra allowance by restarting a room or reinstalling. At most one trial-sponsored room and one connected owner-granted program may be active at a time; program and room identities must be explicit. A guest still needs their own verified account, current membership and any applicable consent; the program owner grants exact actions independently. Trial or payment status never grants the effect by itself.

The [conditional Minecraft target](2026-09-19-conditional-minecraft-paid-action-owner-selection-46.md) remains subject to delegated guest admission, installed native evidence and commercial-rights disposition. If a non-game fallback is selected, the same provisional aggregate limits apply to its accepted bounded-effect unit only after that unit is precisely defined. CFP-1 must still freeze exact action IDs, per-action effect bounds, counter/reset and handoff semantics, trial start/expiry, outage and in-flight settlement, price/cost and final customer notice. Cost measurement and installed concurrency tests may require an explicit owner-approved revision before release; no silence or test result changes these provisional values automatically.
